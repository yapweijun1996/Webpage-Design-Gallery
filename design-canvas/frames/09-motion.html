09 / Design system

Motion

Match the timing to the distance and the action.

System motion is enabledReduced motion is on. Changes happen instantly.

Track · marker · trigger

Timing in action

  • Fast --dc-dur-fast · 120 ms
  • Base --dc-dur · 220 ms
  • Slow --dc-dur-slow · 400 ms
  • Linear --dc-dur-slow · 400 ms

The first three tracks use --dc-ease to settle into place. Compare Slow and Linear to feel the difference at the same duration.

Do use fast timing for feedback and reserve slow timing for larger moves. Avoid making people wait for an animation before they can act.

Surface · label · focus ring

Hover & press

Hover, press, or tab to the button.

Hover changes the fill. Press shifts the button while held. Keyboard focus adds a ring. The disabled button stays still.

Do pair motion with a visible state. Avoid relying on movement alone to confirm an action.
