Frequently asked questions

Which plan includes a cash-flow forecast?

Growth and Scale show your projected cash balance up to 13 weeks ahead. Starter gives you a weekly cash summary.

Do I need to know accounting?

You can start with what you already know: how much cash you have, what customers owe you and which bills are due.

Can I start if I keep my books in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Have your current balances, unpaid invoices and upcoming bills handy so you can see what needs to carry over.

Can I adjust the forecast if a customer pays late?

Yes. Move the expected payment date to see how the delay could affect the cash you have available for upcoming bills.

Can I try Harbor Ledger before choosing a plan?

Yes. Start a free 14-day trial with a typical week of sales and bills. Check your cash position, then come back the following week to see what changed.

What happens in the demo?

In 20 minutes, you’ll see how to check your cash position and update a forecast. Bring one decision you need to make about cash this week, such as whether to pay a supplier now or after a customer pays.
