Know what you can spend between paydays

  • Know what’s available today

    Check your balances before committing to a purchase.

  • Spot a tight week early

    Look ahead to see whether you can place a stock order this week or should wait.

  • Prioritize overdue invoices

    Use invoice due dates to decide which customers to contact first.

  • Plan for bills before they’re due

    Set aside money for supplier payments and regular expenses.

  • Spend less time sorting expenses

    Group expenses by category so you can find a transaction when a question comes up.

  • Review the month with your accountant

    Use a monthly summary to discuss where your money went.
