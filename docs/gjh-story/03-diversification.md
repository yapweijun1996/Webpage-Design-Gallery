# Goh Joo Hin’s diversification into health and electronics

**Evidence key.** **CONFIRMED** means one official source or at least two independent sources support the stated claim. An attributed company milestone confirms what the company records. **REPORTED** means one non-official source. **UNVERIFIED** marks a claim the available evidence leaves open.

## Borsch Med and the move into health

**CONFIRMED — 2004 brand milestone.** Goh Joo Hin (GJH) dates its entry into health supplements to Borsch Med, which it calls its fifth brand. The brand history describes a registration in 2004 and puts Borsch Med’s appointment for Pien Tze Huang in the same year. It dates the Lingzhi Cracked Spores Powder Capsule to 2007 and the Cordyceps Sinensis Powder Capsule to 2008. A May 2010 advertisement archived by NewspaperSG shows both products being marketed by then. [GJH corporate timeline](https://www.gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [Borsch Med history](https://www.gjh.com.sg/borsch-med-history/) · [The Straits Times, 20 May 2010, p. 17](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/digitised/issue/straitstimes20100520-1)

**CONFIRMED — the two dates describe different records.** The published copy of ACRA’s entity record for Borsch Med Pte. Ltd., UEN 200006993N, gives **10 August 2000** as the legal entity’s incorporation date and records a former name, ADTIVEMEDIA.COM. Borsch Med’s own 2024 release describes Borsch Med Pte Ltd as established in **2004**, while GJH’s brand history calls 2004 the brand registration year. Use 2004 for the health-brand milestone and 2000 for the legal entity’s incorporation. **UNVERIFIED —** the date the entity took its current name, the specific registration behind the 2004 brand milestone, and GJH’s shareholding in Borsch Med Pte. Ltd. [ACRA corporate dataset](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_3a3807c023c61ddfba947dc069eb53f2/view) · [ACRA-derived entity record](https://opengovsg.com/corporate/200006993N) · [Borsch Med history](https://www.gjh.com.sg/borsch-med-history/) · [Borsch Med April 2024 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/borsch-med-introduces-chondroitin-supplements-to-singapore-for-healthy-joints-302124164.html)

**CONFIRMED — documented management and sales roles.** Borsch Med says GJH personnel operate and manage the business. Its April 2024 release identifies Borsch Med Pte Ltd as the appointed Singapore distributor for a Zeria chondroitin product line. In November it named selected Unity pharmacies as retail outlets for those products. GJH also lists Borsch Med-branded capsules in its own eShop. [April distribution announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/borsch-med-introduces-chondroitin-supplements-to-singapore-for-healthy-joints-302124164.html) · [November Unity announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/borsch-med-partners-with-unity-pharmacies-to-help-elderly-access-zeria-chondroitin-based-joint-health-supplements-in-support-of-healthy-ageing-in-singapore-302318175.html) · [GJH eShop](https://www.gjheshop.com/)

## Electronics timeline and documented roles

**CONFIRMED — Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd operates the eShop.** Its terms govern purchases through that storefront. A product listing there documents a direct retail offer for that item. [GJH eShop terms](https://www.gjheshop.com/terms-and-conditions/)

GJH’s eShop also lists an A270 handset. A 2004 Siemens manual already lists Singapore customer support. **UNVERIFIED —** current exclusivity or a specific territory beyond Singapore.
