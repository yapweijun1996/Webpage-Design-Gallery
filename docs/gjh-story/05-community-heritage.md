# Goh Joo Hin — community, heritage and public record

**CONFIRMED** means an official record or at least two independent sources support the stated fact. **REPORTED** means a single non-official source supports it. **UNVERIFIED** marks a detail the available records leave open. Company material establishes what Goh Joo Hin (GJH) publicly says about its own history.

## The New Moon Big Walk and community giving

**CONFIRMED.** On 1 August 2010, North East Community Development Council (CDC) and GJH organised the New Moon Big Walk. The CDC reports about 5,000 participants and more than 500,000 bowls’ worth of rice, valued at S$85,000, for families in need in the North East district. Its report includes a participant photograph. NewspaperSG indexes an event listing in that day’s *The New Paper* and reserves the issue’s scans for library terminals. [CDC FY2010 annual report, printed p. 14](https://file.go.gov.sg/cdcar-2010.pdf#page=16) · [*The New Paper*, 1 August 2010, “What’s On”](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/digitised/issue/newpaper20100801-1)

**REPORTED.** A contemporary event notice posted on a personal blog describes a 5 km loop starting and ending by White Sands and Pasir Ris interchange. **CONFIRMED.** The CDC and GJH also established a S$200,000 Seniors Healthcare Fund for seniors in need in the district. **UNVERIFIED.** The fund’s contribution split, individual grants and later operation remain open. Use the rice valuation and fund amount in separate captions. [May 2010 event notice](https://shilianglim.wordpress.com/2010/05/23/newmoon-big-walk-2010/) · [CDC annual report](https://file.go.gov.sg/cdcar-2010.pdf#page=16)

**CONFIRMED.** The Dyslexia Association of Singapore names GJH (New Moon) among its major donors in FY2019–20. **REPORTED.** In November 2023, 8world reported that Goh Hock Siang committed S$2 million in his personal capacity to the Che Sen Khor welfare fund, scheduled as S$200,000 annually over ten years. **UNVERIFIED.** The interview establishes the announced schedule, while actual payments over the full term remain unverified. [DAS group annual report](https://das.org.sg/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/das-Group-Annual-Report2020.pdf) · [8world interview](https://www.8world.com/community-events/new-moon-che-sen-khor-2293996)

## SG60, awards and the festive table

**CONFIRMED.** The Edge Singapore’s SG60 Heritage series carries GJH’s own account, *Tradition that Inspires Tomorrow*. The feature captions a photograph as the old GJH shophouse, describes the business as family-owned and professionally managed, and places New Moon’s entry into its portfolio in the mid-1980s. **REPORTED.** The 2026 8world obituary describes GJH as launching New Moon as its first house brand in 1959, echoing the company timeline. **UNVERIFIED.** The exact acquisition date and original ownership remain open. Keep the 1959 brand milestone and the SG60 account’s portfolio milestone separately attributed. [GJH’s SG60 feature](https://www.theedgesingapore.com/SG60/heritage/tradition-inspires-tomorrow) · [GJH timeline](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [8world obituary](https://www.8world.com/singapore/chairman-of-new-moon-abalone-pass-away-3248591)

**CONFIRMED.** A 30 August 2005 *Lianhe Zaobao* clipping preserved in fellow recipient Stamford Tyres’ annual report lists New Moon among the inaugural Singapore Heritage Brand Award group. The Singapore Manufacturers’ Federation records New Moon Abalone Yusheng as the 2006 Singapore Packaging Star Best Entry for Commercial Sales and Display. A 2016 release records the New Moon Abalone & Wine Gift Set as a triple winner across Singapore Packaging Star, AsiaStar and WorldStar. [*Lianhe Zaobao* clipping, printed p. 27](https://investor.stamfordtyres.com/misc/ar2006.pdf) · [2006 packaging results, PDF p. 8](https://www.smfederation.org.sg/sites/default/files/smf/emagazine/newsdec06.pdf#page=8) · [2016 packaging release](https://backup.manufacturing.org.sg/sites/default/files/smf/press-releases/160705Sg_PCS_Awards_Press_Release_0.pdf) · [AsiaStar 2015 packaging catalogue](https://www.asianpackaging.org/wp-content/uploads/awards-winner/winner-2015/Consumer%20Package_2015.pdf)

**CONFIRMED.** GJH lists an SG50 trade mark award, and the Korea Institute of Intellectual Property names New Moon among the five marks honoured at IPOS’s 2015 awards. **UNVERIFIED.** GJH’s Singapore Superbrands listing gives 2002–04. The corresponding awarding-body rolls were unavailable in this review. [GJH awards](https://www.gjhglobal.com/awards-recognition/) · [KIIP’s August 2015 bulletin](https://www.kiip.re.kr/issuefocus/pdf/Issue_Focus_20150828.pdf)

**CONFIRMED.** Singapore Chinese Cultural Centre describes yusheng and pencai as dishes that have grown in popularity at Chinese New Year reunions alongside dishes specific to individual communities. A Bank of China advertisement in *TODAY* offered a New Moon Classic gift set with Australian abalone for Lunar New Year in January 2012. The official GJH eShop lists a New Moon abalone treasure pot, giving the story a present-day counterpart to that festive marketing. [Culturepaedia](https://culturepaedia.singaporeccc.org.sg/popular-culture/traditional-chinese-new-year-dishes-in-singapore/) · [*TODAY*, 16 January 2012, p. 11, NewspaperSG](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/digitised/issue/today20120116-1) · [GJH eShop](https://www.gjheshop.com/brand/new-moon/)

**REPORTED.** In January 2018, *Lianhe Zaobao* interviewed GJH’s chairman for a story on festive abalone prices. The paper found that several households it interviewed still preferred higher-priced abalone. A panel can show customers comparing tins and origins, with the scene framed as an illustration of that reporting. [*Lianhe Zaobao*, 24 January 2018](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/sme/news/story20180124-829515)

## Leadership and succession

**CONFIRMED.** Goh Hock Siang, also recorded as Goh Kai Kia, was GJH’s second-generation chairman and died on 13 August 2026. The 2024 Government Gazette lists him under Goh Kai Kia as a Public Service Medal recipient and patron of the Joo Chiat Community Club Management Committee. The indexed excerpt of a sponsored January 2023 interview names Goh Kai Kui as CEO. AsiaOne identifies him as the chairman’s son and third-generation CEO in August 2026. **UNVERIFIED.** The formal appointment date and any subsequent chairmanship remain open. [Government Gazette, 2024, Public Service Medal entry 54](https://www.pmo.gov.sg/-/media/PMO/National-Day-Awards/NDA_PDF/NDA-2024-pdf.ashx) · [*Lianhe Zaobao* obituary](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/singapore/story20260813-9516010) · [AsiaOne obituary](https://www.asiaone.com/singapore/goh-hock-siang-chairman-new-moon-abalone-singapore-distributor-dies) · [Vulcan Post sponsored interview](https://vulcanpost.com/814208/new-moon-abalone-singapore/)

**CONFIRMED as GJH’s account.** Its timeline credits the Goh brothers with starting the Rochor Road provision store. **REPORTED.** *Lianhe Zaobao* says the chairman’s father took over an existing shop named Yu Xing and added the family surname. AsiaOne names the father as Goh Yeow Gek and dates the takeover to the 1940s. **UNVERIFIED.** The sequence connecting the takeover to the brothers’ account remains open. [GJH timeline](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [*Lianhe Zaobao* interview, 2024](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/singapore/story20240816-4481552) · [AsiaOne obituary](https://www.asiaone.com/singapore/goh-hock-siang-chairman-new-moon-abalone-singapore-distributor-dies)
