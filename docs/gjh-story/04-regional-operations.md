# Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd — Regional distribution and operations

Research checked 20 November 2026. **CONFIRMED** means an official source supports the claim as phrased, or at least two independent sources support it. **REPORTED** means one non-official source supports it. **UNVERIFIED** marks a claim the sources leave open.

**CONFIRMED — source overlap.** GJH’s two corporate domains publish the same timeline and count as one source. [GJH Global](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [GJH Singapore](https://gjh.com.sg/about-us/)

## The move into exports

**CONFIRMED — GJH’s account.** The corporate timeline places its first steps toward exports under **1982**, linking them to GOGO canned drinks and Flying Wheel abalone. GJH’s own SG60 feature in *The Edge Singapore* places both brands in the early 1980s and associates them with the move into exports. The feature carries GJH’s byline. [Corporate timeline](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [GJH’s SG60 account](https://www.theedgesingapore.com/SG60/heritage/tradition-inspires-tomorrow)

**UNVERIFIED — exact launch and shipment chronology.** The GOGO page dates its creation to **1986** and describes canned drinks as mainly an export business at the outset. The Flying Wheel page gives **2004** as its founding year. These accounts conflict with the corporate timeline’s placement of both brands under 1982. The sources leave the first shipment’s date, destination, cargo and buyer open. [GOGO](https://gjhglobal.com/gogo/) · [Flying Wheel](https://gjhglobal.com/flying-wheel/) · [Corporate timeline](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/)

## Markets, distribution and warehousing

**CONFIRMED — GJH’s published service footprint.** GJH describes a distributor network and a warehousing and distribution system that supplies inventory across the region. It names Brunei, Cambodia, China, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam as examples. A product-level check in Malaysia found New Moon 425 g New Zealand abalone on Watsons Malaysia’s marketplace under the seller name “New Moon Official,” and a separate 425 g New Zealand abalone listing at Hai-O. Casio Music SG’s terms identify GJH as the appointed distributor for Casio electronic musical instruments and describe Singapore courier fulfilment and returns to a GJH warehouse. [GJH corporate profile](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/) · [Watsons Malaysia](https://www.watsons.com.my/new-moon-new-zealand-abalone-wild-caught-425g/p/BP_7000002214) · [Hai-O](https://mall.hai-o.com.my/e00400479) · [Casio Music SG terms](https://casiomusic.sg/pages/terms-conditions)

**REPORTED — wider reach and a shipment example.** AsiaOne reports a presence in at least 17 other countries without naming them. Commercial trade-data provider NBD lists July 2026 exports from GJH to Vietnam for New Moon chilli crab paste and Australian abalone. **UNVERIFIED** are the full current market-by-product roster, distributor names and exclusivity by territory, and the network’s warehouse count and capacity. [AsiaOne](https://www.asiaone.com/singapore/goh-hock-siang-chairman-new-moon-abalone-singapore-distributor-dies) · [NBD shipment listings](https://en.nbd.ltd/trader/info/NBDML104549401) · [GJH corporate profile](https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/)

## Singapore and Johor offices

**CONFIRMED — Singapore.** GJH and HPB both list **8 Gul Avenue, Singapore 629652**. A Philippine trademark recordal dated 28 December 2020 records an address change from **2 Link Road** to **8 Gul Avenue** for a New Moon filing. That date belongs to the trademark recordal. A GJH recruitment listing at Gul Avenue describes picking and packing products into cartons for e-commerce. [GJH contact page](https://gjhglobal.com/contact-us/) · [HPB supplier entry](https://www.hpb.gov.sg/schools/school-programmes/healthy-meals-suppliers-list/goh-joo-hin-pte-ltd/) · [IPOPHL address recordal](https://onlineservices.ipophil.gov.ph/tmgazette/Unlimited/Attachment/RECORDAL_20210107.PDF) · [GJH job posting](https://www.fastjobs.sg/singapore-job-ad/2656021/rec-job)

**CONFIRMED — Malaysia office as listed.** GJH gives **No. 176 Jalan Ekoperniagaan 5, Taman Ekoperniagaan 2, Senai Airport City, 81400 Senai, Johor, Malaysia**. For 2022, the Malaysian Pineapple Industry Board separately lists **Goh Joo Hin (Kuala Lumpur) Sdn. Bhd.** as an exporter with a registered address in **Kulai, Johor**. **UNVERIFIED** are the legal entity occupying the Senai office, its opening date and any warehouse or airport cargo role at that address. [GJH contact page](https://gjhglobal.com/contact-us/) · [Malaysian Pineapple Industry Board exporter list](https://www.mpib.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Data-Nanas-2022.pdf)

## Trademark protection and sales channels

**CONFIRMED — company-stated trademark footprint.** The New Moon page says GJH’s brands are registered and protected in 17 markets. Its list includes Argentina, Australia, Brunei, Cambodia, China, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia/Sarawak, Mexico, Myanmar, Peru, the Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan and Vietnam. The MILI page gives the same count with a different list, including France, Sri Lanka and Thailand. Cambodia’s gazette records GJH’s New Moon device mark in Class 29 (registration 20356), and the Philippine gazette lists a historical New Moon registration in Classes 29 and 30 (4/2012/00010906). **UNVERIFIED** are the current status and goods covered by every registration behind the company’s count. [New Moon](https://gjhglobal.com/newmoon/) · [MILI](https://www.gjhglobal.com/mili/) · [Cambodia trademark gazette, printed pp. 279–280](https://dip.cambodiaip.gov.kh/DocResources/87d96005-445c-4e76-b06b-d649f885b144_e9e18593-8994-4a44-818e-1c9f313dbe17-kh.pdf#page=279) · [Philippine registrations gazette](https://onlineservices.ipophil.gov.ph/tmgazette/Unlimited/Attachment/REGISTERED_20150706.PDF)

**CONFIRMED — three buyer routes.** Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd (UEN 199306282E) operates the multibrand eShop and uses logistics partners for order delivery. The corporate New Moon channel advertises staff wellness bundles, charity care packs and bulk gifting. HPB lists GJH in the West region of its Healthy Meals Suppliers List, with products such as MILI Vegetarian Oyster Sauce (510 g) and New Moon Singapore Laksa Paste (24 × 100 g). HPB asks school and preschool buyers to check that purchases still meet programme guidelines. [eShop terms](https://www.gjheshop.com/terms-and-conditions/) · [eShop catalogue](https://www.gjheshop.com/shop/) · [Corporate sales](https://gjhglobal.com/corporate-sales/) · [GJH supplier entry](https://www.hpb.gov.sg/schools/school-programmes/healthy-meals-suppliers-list/goh-joo-hin-pte-ltd/) · [HPB directory guidance](https://www.hpb.gov.sg/schools/school-programmes/healthy-meals-suppliers-list/)
