# V11 stage 9 — mall theme infrastructure

Status at the stage 9 snapshot: local implementation in the opt-in expanded preview, with **Everyday** as the only theme. Stage 10 subsequently added [Lunar New Year](gjh-v11-stage10-lunar-new-year.md), [Christmas](gjh-v11-stage10-christmas.md) and [Deepavali](gjh-v11-stage10-deepavali.md).

## Visitor experience

Visitors can open **Theme** in the 3D scene or **Mall theme settings** in the expanded store directory. The native dialog names the current selection, exposes a keyboard-operable select and Apply action, announces its result and returns focus to its opener. A modal theme panel pauses movement without stowing a cart, standing a seated visitor, dropping a physical product, losing a demo-bag selection or resetting an elevator journey. The original layout does not expose an unsupported theme setting. The expanded V1 visitor can use Everyday, although its existing lift limitation is unchanged.

**Automatic (follows the calendar)** is the default. Selecting a specific theme, including Everyday, saves an override under the browser-local key `gjh-v11-mall-theme-v1`. Applying Automatic removes that override. An unsupported stored ID is treated as Automatic. If storage is unavailable, the choice continues in memory for the current session. Mall themes are independent of the interface's light/dark preference. The calendar controls and status are available in English, Simplified Chinese and Malay.

### Festival calendar

The [festival calendar](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/festival-calendar.js) uses the visitor's local civil date. Decorations begin 42 days before a festival and stay through the third day after it, inclusive. Each festival can override either window. If windows overlap, the nearest upcoming festival takes priority. When only tails overlap, the most recent festival takes priority. Between windows, Everyday is active and the dialog names the next known festival. For 2026, Deepavali's tail ends on 11 November, 12 November is Everyday, and Christmas starts on 13 November.

Startup waits for the selected theme before exposing the first 3D frame. The automatic choice is checked again on return to the tab, on `pageshow` and at local midnight. If a decoration pack fails to load, the current appearance remains available and Automatic can retry on the next calendar check.

- Christmas is 25 December. Lunar New Year uses the first day of the first month from `Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-u-ca-chinese')`, with a 2026–2035 fallback from the Hong Kong Observatory. The supplied dates for 2027 and 2030 take precedence because ICU 76.1 returned 7 February 2027 and 2 February 2030. The published dates are 6 February and 3 February respectively. [Hong Kong Observatory conversion tables](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/conversion.htm), [Hong Kong Observatory on the 2030 new moon](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/Observatorys-Blog/109092/No-Lunar-New-Year%E2%80%99s-Eve-on-the-30th-Day-for-Five-Consecutive-Years), [Singapore MOM 2027 calendar](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0618-public-holidays-for-2027)
- Deepavali's 2026 date, 8 November, is confirmed by Singapore MOM. The calendar keeps 29 October 2027 for the specified Lakshmi Puja observance. Singapore's gazetted Deepavali holiday is 28 October that year. [Singapore MOM 2026 calendar](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2025/0616-public-holidays-for-2026), [Singapore MOM 2027 calendar](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0618-public-holidays-for-2027), [Singapore PanchangTime calendar](https://panchangtime.com/festivals/diwali/singapore)
- The 2028–2035 Deepavali entries are provisional for Singapore. The supplied dates for 2028–2033 and 2035 match the Lakshmi Puja reference. Regional calendars can vary by a day, including 2031 (13 November in the Southern Deepavali calendar) and 2034 (10 November in the Lakshmi Puja reference). The mall uses the supplied 9 November 2034 date, which agrees with the Southern Deepavali calendar. Outside the table, Deepavali is omitted and Everyday applies between the other festival windows. [Lakshmi Puja dates](https://diwali.info/diwali-dates), [Q++ Southern Deepavali calendar](https://www.qppstudio.net/global-holidays-observances/southern-deepavali-diwali-krisna-chaturdasi.htm)

## Theme ownership and lifecycle

[The local theme registry and coordinator](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/mall-themes.js) own the selected ID, allowed definitions, status and persistence. User-controlled storage never supplies scripts, URLs or materials. The Everyday definition owns named scene roles for stone, wood, green, ivory, brass, glass and light colors, plus corresponding expanded-lift materials. [The scene adapter](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/world.js) validates its roles before applying them; the lift has its own validated adapter. Brand images, pinned GLB materials, interactions, geometry and collision are outside this initial color owner.

Preparation is separated from synchronous commit. A generation token and `AbortSignal` reject stale work; a failed preparation or commit keeps the previous active state, with the scene adapter attempting rollback. Staged resources provide a cleanup callback and are released on replacement, cancellation or destruction. Closing settings, hiding the document or losing WebGL cancels pending preparation. On actual graphics recovery, the current definition is reapplied before the renderer is declared ready. Reapplying Everyday updates the existing materials/lights without making another scene, texture or set of listeners.

At the stage 9 snapshot there were no themed decoration assets to load or replace; the alternative themes used in its coordinator tests were synthetic test records only. Stage 10 implements and tests its first real pack separately.

If the optional theme module cannot load, the stage 8 Everyday appearance and the mall remain available; the settings panel reports that theme settings are unavailable. During graphics loss the theme panel closes, settings are unavailable in the fallback directory, and the existing graphics recovery path controls re-entry. A no-JavaScript directory remains readable without a misleading settings button.
