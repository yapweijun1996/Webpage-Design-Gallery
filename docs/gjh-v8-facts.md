## Suggested V8 timeline

Use the following five milestones because each is directly represented on the official About Us page. The text is intentionally concise so that the page does not turn a company self-description into an invented historical narrative.

1. **1940s — Rochor Road beginnings.** The Goh brothers started a provision store supplying daily food essentials.
2. **1959 — New Moon brand origin.** New Moon’s own history dates the brand’s establishment and first abalone product to 1959; the date should not be read as a GJH ownership date.
3. **1981 — MILI.** The second house brand was introduced with canned foods.
4. **1982 — Initial exports.** The company timeline places GOGO canned drinks and Flying Wheel Japanese abalones among the first export steps.
5. **2004 — Health portfolio.** Borsch Med is described as the fifth brand and a Traditional Chinese Medicine supplement business.

The official About Us page also describes later milestones in 2006 (Gigaset), 2011 (Sony personal entertainment products), 2016 (GP Batteries), 2018 (Casio and LG), 2021 (Aeries air purifier), 2024 (joint-care supplements) and 2025 (SteelSeries distribution partnership). V8 should use these only if the copy links to the official page and retains the wording as a reported company milestone.
