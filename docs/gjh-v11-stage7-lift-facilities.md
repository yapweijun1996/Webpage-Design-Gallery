# V11 stage 7 — expanded lift and facilities

This page records the Stage 7 baseline. The current public layout is documented in [Expanded V11 public facilities](gjh-v11-public-facilities.md).

Status: local expanded-layout implementation and verification. The default scene and its existing experimental lift remain unchanged. The expanded preview uses a separate visitor-only lift and a small upper viewing area, not a second complete retail floor.

## Spatial changes

The [stage 1 plan](gjh-v11-stage1-spatial-plan.md) places the lift shaft at plan `(20,36)–(24,40)` and the 12 × 12 m upper landing at `(16,32)–(28,44)`. The actual expanded layout uses `worldX = planX − 25` and `worldZ = 48 − planY`; the shaft is therefore centered at `(-3,10)`, and the upper landing spans `x=-9…3`, `z=4…16` at +7.2 m. The stage 1 document's older alternative world conversion and its stated `(-13,-8)` center predate this final translation; the plan coordinates themselves are unchanged. These virtual dimensions are design assumptions, not a measured property or a building-code assessment.

The lift now has an aligned west-facing opening on both levels, a 4 × 4 m void surrounded by four upper-floor slabs, a moving cabin, collision-protected perimeter rails and a small walkable viewing circuit. A collision platform supports the shaft only when the cabin is at the upper landing. The landing gates are closed while the cabin is absent or moving; the player controller owns the rider during travel. On graphics interruption the existing recovery selects the nearest landing, restores its gate/platform state and re-enables the visitor capsule. The interaction automatically calls the cabin to the visitor's floor and positions the visitor at its center before travelling; it is a demo transition, not animated boarding, a timed call system or a simulation of physical lift doors.

To keep the ground-floor lobby clear, the directory kiosk moved to `(-5,44)` and the self-service guest desk to `(25,44)` near the entrance. The New Moon showcase moved to `(4,11)` with its four displayed items and interaction aligned to the cabinet. A closed, neutral future-facilities reservation occupies the eastern interior at `(25,10)`; it has no restroom, plumbing, operational services or public entrance. The map describes the lift and planned facilities; it continues to link only to the three open stores. Stage 1's north-corner service reserves remain closed with no asserted loading route. The ring and garden remain separate from these fixtures. The expanded courtyard arrival was also moved to `(10,36)`: the original `(-2,12)` lay inside the new shaft boundary and prevented safe cart activation.

The lift rejects a pushed cart, a seated visitor or a carried physical product; the demo bag is independent and remains intact. Visitor v1 retains the ground-floor scene and store journeys but does not have the V2 lift; the directory and map say so. Normal maps can still return a visitor from the upper level to an existing store. Seasonal decorations and additional brand stores remain out of scope.
