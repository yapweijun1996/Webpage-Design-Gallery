## Visitor story

A side panel introduces MILI as a Goh Joo Hin house brand launched in 1981 and shows how its packaged pantry products cover fruits, vegetables, vegetarian food, meat, seafood and sauces. At the cookline, visitors can compare the two officially listed 510 g sauces. GJH lists oyster extract in the regular sauce and mushroom extract in the vegetarian version. A small pantry marker links to the vegetarian mock-duck listing. The display copy uses the official catalogue and recognises each distinct product without depicting MILI as a noodle manufacturer. [GJH history](https://gjh.com.sg/about-us/) · [MILI Oyster Sauce](https://www.gjheshop.com/product/mili-oyster-sauce-510g/) · [MILI Vegetarian Oyster Sauce](https://www.gjheshop.com/product/mili-vegetarian-oyster-sauce-510g/)
