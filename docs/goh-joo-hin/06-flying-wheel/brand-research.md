# S06 · Flying Wheel brand research

Evidence checked 2026-11-19. Product imagery is linked to the first-party source where available.

- The GJH corporate brand page centers abalone. GJH eShop verifies Flying Wheel All-Natural Air-Dried Singapore Ban Mee Noodles 70g, and also lists Shanghai LaMian, Japanese Ramen, abalone and bird's nest products. JAKIM separately corroborates the three air-dried noodle categories. The Ban Mee product listing identifies Malaysia as origin, despite “Singapore” in the product name. The corporate timeline and brand page have inconsistent dates. [Flying Wheel corporate](https://gjh.com.sg/flying-wheel/) · [GJH eShop brand range](https://www.gjheshop.com/brand/flying-wheel/) · [Ban Mee 70g](https://www.gjheshop.com/product/bundle-of-6-flying-wheel-all-natural-air-dried-singapore-ban-mee-noodles-70g/) · [JAKIM product register](https://www.halal.gov.my/directory/slm_viewdetail.php?comp_code=COMP-20090616-154615&ty=CO&type=C)
- The corporate eShop SKU result and category page identify the 70 g product. The direct page returned a verification challenge on a subsequent open, so use the brand listing and JAKIM as independent evidence. The in-repo source notes preserve the previously reviewed listing. No exact packaging art use rights found.
