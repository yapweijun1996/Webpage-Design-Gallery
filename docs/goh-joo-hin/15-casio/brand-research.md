# S15 · Casio brand research

Evidence checked 2026-11-19. Product imagery is linked to the first-party source where available.

- Casio Music SG identifies Goh Joo Hin as its appointed distributor for electronic musical instruments. Its storefront lists the Casiotone CT-S1 and CELVIANO Grand Hybrid GP-510. Casio Singapore documents the CT-S1BK as a black, 61-key digital keyboard, 930 × 258 × 83 mm without the music stand. The GP-510BP is a polished-black cabinet-form digital hybrid with 3 pedals and a piano bench, 1434 × 489 × 963 mm without the music stand. Casio also provides a first-party AR reference for the GP-510BP. [Distributor terms](https://casiomusic.sg/pages/terms-conditions), [GJH-operated CT-S1 listing](https://casiomusic.sg/products/ct-s1), [GJH-operated digital piano listing](https://casiomusic.sg/collections/digital-piano), [Casio CT-S1BK](https://www.casio.com/sg/electronic-musical-instruments/product.CT-S1BK/), [Casio GP-510BP](https://www.casio.com/sg/electronic-musical-instruments/product.GP-510BP/), [Casio Music SG AR references](https://casiomusic.sg/pages/casio-music-ar-augmented-reality)
- V11 has `s15-grand-piano-{low,balanced}.glb`, a second `gallery-assets/grand-piano.glb`, the brand-neutral `electronic-keyboard.glb` (61 keys, blank display) and `s15-recital-details.glb`. The curved, open-lid acoustic grand is an original stage fixture. It materially differs from the GP-510BP cabinet and from the CT-S1BK. The generic 61-key keyboard is a useful visual starting point whose control and speaker layout needs matching before naming it CT-S1BK.
