# S17 · Gigaset brand research

Evidence checked 2026-11-19. Product imagery is linked to the first-party source where available.

- GJH identifies A/C/E/S-class DECT phone distribution in Singapore at the range level. The manufacturer verifies the A170 as a cordless analog DECT model; its gallery and manual document the handset/cradle and the product page gives handset dimensions 159 × 48 × 30 mm. Gigaset's COMFORT 550A is another verified analog DECT handset with an answering-machine base and a 2.2-inch TFT. These manufacturer references establish products and visual form, with GJH's page covering series rather than current inventory for either specific model. [GJH Gigaset](https://www.gjh.com.sg/gigaset/), [Gigaset A170](https://www.gigaset.com/en_en/gigaset-a170/), [Gigaset A170/A270 manual](https://gse.gigaset.com/fileadmin/legacy-assets/CustomerCare/Manuals/A1xx_A2xx_A3xx_AL1xx_AL2xx_AL3xx/A170-A270A/A31008-M2802-B101-1-4N19_en_GR-IE-MEA-UK-International.pdf), [Gigaset COMFORT 550A](https://www.gigaset.com/hq_en/gigaset-comfort-550/)
- V11 has `s17-telephone-alcove-{low,balanced,detailed}.glb`, `cordless-phone-display.glb`, `gallery-assets/lounge-sofa-bench.glb` and `s17-telephone-details.glb`.
