# S01 · New Moon brand research

Goh Joo Hin dates the launch of New Moon, its first house brand, to 1959. The first product was abalone. The company records later launches in bird’s nest, chicken essence, rice, sauces, pastes, chilled and frozen foods, and other groceries. The Singapore eShop documents the wider range today. [New Moon history](https://gjh.com.sg/newmoon-history/) · [GJH corporate history](https://gjh.com.sg/about-us/) · [GJH eShop brand catalogue](https://www.gjheshop.com/brand/new-moon/)

## Proposed educational journey

| Stop | What the visitor learns | Source and design consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left entry wall | Abalone was the first New Moon product in 1959. The brand later expanded its pantry and wellness categories. | Use a short interactive heritage panel. Present the launch as a date and sourced fact. The retro-styled illustration may use original graphics, with no invented archival scene or unverified award seal. [Brand history](https://gjh.com.sg/newmoon-history/) |
| Four rear cabinets | Four different products and origins sit side by side. | Give the products the strongest sightline from the threshold. Retain accurate model identity, package colours and native can scale. Let the optional existing Inspect reveal close-up and original photography. [GJH abalone category](https://www.gjheshop.com/product-category/abalone/) |
| Side tasting island | The brand shows New Zealand abalone served in steamboat, soups and cold dishes. | Use three small serving illustrations and concise text from the brand’s presentation. Place the optional action on the aisle side, leaving both existing stools and the main aisle clear. [NewMoonSG serving video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fc7GHn69b08) |
| Product details | Visitors can follow an official product or store link. | Preserve the current Demo Bag and external official path. Shopify and payment integration are future work. [GJH official eShop](https://www.gjheshop.com/) |

The crescent wordmark and a pink pack with scalloped graphics appear in dated retail advertising. Treat this as evidence of the 2024 campaign edition. It supports a restrained crescent or scalloped framing device above the product bays, with label art sourced independently per SKU. Historical price, stock and promotional claims do not carry into the mall. NewspaperSG indexes a FairPrice advertisement naming New Moon New Zealand abalone in the 25 January 2008 Straits Times, page 18. The page image requires library access, so the indexed text establishes retail presence and leaves the package artwork unverified. ## Design and rights status

The floor plan and placement of the four cabinets are retained. Prior architectural illustrations and sculpted triptych exports remain design drafts. The light proposal starts from the entry sightline and native-scale product hierarchy, then uses a cream and honey oak backdrop with a yellow crescent. Keep optional interactions out of the central aisle and test signage at player height. The clear central lane and wider rear browse strip are design targets for this virtual shop. [BCA Universal Design Guide, supermarkets and retail outlets](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/docs/default-source/universaldesign/udguide2016.pdf) · [Enterprise Singapore visual merchandising guide](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/-/media/esg/files/non-financial-assistance/for-companies/growth-partnership-programme/heartlands-go-digital/hgd_visual_merchandising.pdf)

Reference links serve research. Publication of new artwork or packaging needs documented source and use rights. No new package GLBs are planned for the four S01 shelves.
