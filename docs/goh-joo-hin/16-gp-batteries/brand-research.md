# S16 · GP Batteries brand research

Evidence checked 2026-11-19. Product imagery is linked to the first-party source where available.

- GJH identifies GP as a primary and rechargeable battery maker. The manufacturer lists GP Ultra+ Alkaline AA, with an illustrated current paper pack, 1.5 V and a 14.5 × 50.5 mm cell. The manufacturer's GP Malaysia catalog also lists the eTag Smart Tracker with a lanyard hole, Apple Find My compatibility and a replaceable CR2032, Ø32 × 8.3 mm. This corroborates the locator theme. The Malaysia listing does not establish Singapore availability. Keep the AA and the eTag's coin cell separate. [GJH GP Batteries](https://www.gjh.com.sg/gp-batteries/), [GP Ultra+ Alkaline AA](https://international.gpbatteries.com/products/gp-ultra-plus-alkaline-aa), [GP Malaysia eTag gallery and specifications](https://my.gpbatteries.com/ms/products/gp-etag-smart-tracker), [GP Malaysia CR2032](https://my.gpbatteries.com/ms/products/gp-lithium-coin-batteries-cr2032)
- V11 has `s16-eyelet-rail-{low,balanced,detailed}.glb`, `battery-display-rack.glb`, `gallery-assets/shelf-electronics-display.glb` and `s16-power-details.glb`. The oversized eyelet rail is an architectural fixture. The battery display rack uses generic blister cards and cylindrical cells, which differ from the current paper-pack reference.
