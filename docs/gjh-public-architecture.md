# GJH public-space architecture

The expanded mall uses a ribbed timber vault and terrazzo runner at the entrance, recessed shopfront lighting on both arcades, glazed clerestory ribs above the lift landing, and a planted wayfinding court around the lift. Ten timber planters along the north and west clerestory carry trailing leaf clusters, echoing A03’s hanging greenery. They share the existing timber and foliage instance batches. The roof ribs are above the upper camera zone. Brand storefronts, the lift threshold, and the public circulation routes retain their original locations.

The freestanding directory beside the west lift entrance is the original **Mall Directory Standee** from the owner's [Blender-3D-Gallery model](https://github.com/yapweijun1996/Blender-3D-Gallery/tree/a87117622ed25efc5f804c9ee6ed0171e4946cf7/models/mall-directory-standee). The unchanged optimized GLB contains 3,316 triangles and five mesh primitives in three materials. It loads asynchronously and retains an ivory marker if the asset cannot be verified. The shape depicts a static wayfinding fixture. The nearby lift, interactive directory and HTML map provide navigation.

The [upstream asset rights](https://github.com/yapweijun1996/Blender-3D-Gallery/blob/a87117622ed25efc5f804c9ee6ed0171e4946cf7/models/mall-directory-standee/LICENSE.md) authorize reuse in the owner's projects. The geometry and materials are original; the upstream author has not specified separate public redistribution terms.

The A01 entrance atrium carries the gallery's [Atrium Chandelier](https://github.com/yapweijun1996/Blender-3D-Gallery/tree/ec02d0889b1f21ac3177c390b81befd34d85b97c/models/atrium-chandelier) beneath the central timber vault rib. Three warm-white light rings, nine suspension rods and 24 opaque faceted pendants share three materials. The original 3 × 2 × 3 m model uses 13,452 triangles and three draws, without a shadow pass or extra point lights. The 459,540-byte texture-free GLB is requested during idle time within 24 m on the ground floor. It is hidden outside that range and on the upper gallery.
