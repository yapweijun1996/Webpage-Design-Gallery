## Recommendation

Choose **B** for the first corporate release. Its category filters, uniform brand cards and separate product panel make a known brand easy to locate and give additional brands a consistent place to go. The selected product and enquiry actions stay together. A gives the company's food heritage more character, with a larger New Moon feature and a stronger Rochor Road spread. Its visual hierarchy gives the other categories less space.

The survey covered v1, v5–v19 using the repository's page structures, thumbnails and desktop/mobile audit. A carries the food-led approach of v5, v12 and v14 into a corporate directory with a named product handoff. B develops the directory approach of v1, v6 and v15 into consistent brand cards with a product-to-enquiry path. Both build on v19's sourcing intent with an editable draft tied to the selected product. The v9–v11 spatial experience becomes a secondary link below About. ### Mobile journey and enquiry handoff

A visitor can tap **New Moon** on the first screen. A jumps to its expanded lead card. B jumps to the dedicated New Moon product panel. **Explore Our Brands** starts at the category controls. Each other named shortcut takes the visitor to the matching card and its **View Brand** link. Category changes filter the cards below the controls. All restores the portfolio. If a shortcut's brand is filtered out, switch to its category before moving focus to its card.

The example is **New Moon Premium Wild Caught New Zealand Abalone 425g**. **View Product** opens the E-Shop listing. **Enquire About This Product** fills the editable Brand and Product fields and moves focus to the enquiry heading. A general enquiry draft starts with empty fields, supports free text and can describe a purchase requirement without a known SKU. Keep any typed purchase requirements when selecting a different product. Navigation **Contact** goes to the enquiry section. The hero's **Sales Enquiry** and the draft's **Open Official Contact** are direct links to GJH's contact page.

GJH's contact page has a **Message** field. The designed handoff is **Copy Enquiry Details** → open official contact → paste into **Message**. A successful copy gets a status announcement. If copy is unavailable, show selectable text with manual-copy instructions and keep the official link usable. If a requested brand is outside the shortlist, offer the official directory and allow manual entry. Both routes operate with the mall unavailable. Measure official contact clicks as clicks. Count completed enquiries once the sales team can attribute one with contact details and a sufficiently specific request.

### Accessibility and verification

- Designed to WCAG AA text contrast. Calculated pairs include A forest on ivory **11.30:1** and terracotta on ivory **5.89:1**, and B navy on ice **15.81:1** and blue on white **7.49:1**. Form borders against their field fill exceed **3:1**.
- Use at least 44 × 44 CSS px for primary actions, filters, shortcuts, card links and navigation, with padding around plain text links. Use visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, heading order and explicit form labels. Convey the active filter in text and programmatically. Give original packshots product-name alt text and hide decorative scenery from assistive technology.
- Checked both mobile layouts at 390 × 844 and reviewed all four page compositions through the footer. The PNGs show design states. Keyboard, filtering, focus, clipboard and external submission require implementation testing. The gallery generator check and 14 unit tests passed. The existing browser suites could not start because Playwright and Pillow were unavailable.
- The official directory and About copy were readable on their official non-www hosts. The www URLs are retained as briefed. Corporate Sales appears in official navigation, but the destination timed out during direct verification. Enquiry prefill support, real submission, sales routing and current packaging approval remain unverified.
