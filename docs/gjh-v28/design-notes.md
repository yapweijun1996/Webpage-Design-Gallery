# GJH v28 design notes

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Introduce the Distribution Desk and give business visitors and shoppers direct routes. |
| Divisions and quick routes | Use wayfinding plaques to open the relevant brand category. Give sales, shop and career visitors one-click destinations. |
| Brand shelf | Let visitors compare Food, Electronics and Health & Nutrition in context using official brand imagery and a text tile for selected Sony products. |
| Trade partners | Explain GJH’s self-described capabilities, name example markets and help visitors draft an enquiry before opening the official contact page. |
| Journey | Give visitors four dated milestones alongside the labelled official-site historical composite. |
| Contact and footer | Hand off to official contact and retain the independent-concept disclosure. |

The [fact notes](../gjh-v8-facts.md) supply the copy guardrails. The categories describe areas of business. The markets are examples, and warehousing and distribution claims are attributed to GJH. The timeline covers the 1940s Rochor Road provision store, New Moon’s 1959 brand origin, MILI as GJH’s second house brand in 1981, and Borsch Med in 2004. The official lifestyle image is captioned without identifying the people as staff. The [asset manifest](../../demos/corporate/gjh/v28/assets/sources.json) ties each derivative to its local source.

The [November ideas study](../gjh-ideas-2026-11/merged-top5-and-image-plan.md) favours one dominant image and a clear task for each visitor. The Receiving Bay informs the buyer checklist, The Pantry Shelf informs category browsing, and Rochor Threshold informs the separation of historical evidence from illustrative design.
