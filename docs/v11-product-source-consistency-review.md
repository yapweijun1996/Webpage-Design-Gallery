**existing verified source data**. Its adjacent text explicitly permits estimated
panel order/spacing and Mexico's disclosed 121-degree blank sector. Acceptance is
four distinguishable products, correct details and accurate limitations.

Earlier audit reports treated physical print masters/full-wrap certification as
mandatory for this checkbox. That exceeded this explicit contract. Independent
review confirmed the correction before acceptance, then separately reviewed the
actual source/render/live proof. The checkbox closes on source consistency; it
does not certify a complete physical replica or erase unknown packaging details.
