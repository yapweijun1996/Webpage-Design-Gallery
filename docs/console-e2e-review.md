### Mobile Auto quality floor

Mobile Auto targets 60 FPS for upgrades and accepts a sustained 24 FPS at its current tier. The controller evaluates foreground frame intervals in windows. It discounts one isolated stall and allows for refresh timing at the window boundary. Tests exercised Balanced and Detailed at 23, 24, 25 and 30 FPS, the two phases of alternating 33.3 and 50 ms intervals that represent 24 FPS on a 60 Hz display, and 60 FPS movement for upgrades. Sustained 23 FPS caused a downgrade. The mobile policy covers viewports up to 650 CSS pixels wide and devices with a coarse primary pointer.

In a mobile Chrome session, real touch input moved the visitor 9.13 metres. A test scheduler skipped selected animation callbacks while preserving their browser timestamps. Measured cadences of 30.00, 28.25 and 23.98 FPS kept Balanced. At 20.00 FPS, Auto reduced the tier to Low and kept the stored preference on Auto. These measurements validate tier decisions under controlled timing. The sustainable FPS on a physical phone still depends on its hardware and the scene.
