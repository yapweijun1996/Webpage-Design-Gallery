## Verification and interpretation

The [four-width drinks runner](../../../tests/browser_yeos_drinks.py) and [edition runner](../../../tests/browser_yeos_editions.py) exercise 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px layouts, JavaScript-disabled content and the visitor controls. Together they refresh each edition’s mobile, tablet and desktop preview and its gallery thumbnail. The [company journey runner](../../../demos/food-beverage/yeos/landing_checks.py) also covers all fifteen editions with keyboard interactions, query state, native disclosures and inter-page handoffs. Its optional axe scan covers the initial main region under WCAG 2 and 2.1 A/AA rules.

The illustrated labels on the cooler and packaging forms are concept art. The actual Singapore product names, availability and volumes remain searchable, selectable HTML. The historical street and river scene is identified as an illustration. The references show desktop compositions and particular app states. On phones the page prioritises readable controls, labelled packs and a reachable primary action. The screenshots document these viewport and state choices in Chrome.
