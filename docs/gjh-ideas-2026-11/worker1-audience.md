## Audience evidence and Singapore benchmarks

GJH has separate corporate and eShop contact pages, publishes career information, and appears in HPB’s school-meals supplier directory. GJH visitor mix remains unmeasured here. Interview each audience and review landing queries before weighting the homepage.

- **Retail.** Sheng Siong names its sourcing team and publishes supplier expectations. FairPrice requests specs and optional images, then routes suppliers through “General Store Feedback.” Put evidence and a clearly named next step together. [Sheng Siong](https://corporate.shengsiong.com.sg/suppliers/) · [FairPrice](https://www.fairpricegroup.com.sg/contact-us/)
- **Foodservice.** Sphere Foods separates retail and kitchen pack formats and confirms terms by enquiry. Its range-level sizes leave SKU-level calculations to buyers. Sin Hwa Dee distinguishes consumer DIY packs from foodservice supply. Make the buying unit explicit. [Sphere Foods](https://spherefoods.com.sg/wholesale-distribution) · [Sin Hwa Dee](https://www.chngkees.com.sg/index.php?information_id=16&route=information%2Finformation)
- **Heritage and distribution.** POKKA dates milestones, Yeo’s uses chapter themes, and Teck Huat captions an archive photograph and separates distribution from brand activation. Pair a specific object with dated evidence. Broad portfolio copy can leave the buyer’s next step unclear. [POKKA](https://pokka.co/about-us/history) · [Yeo’s](https://yeos.com.sg/about-us/history-heritage/) · [Teck Huat](https://www.teckhuat.com/)

## Seven candidates

Each pitch, visitor job and design is [assumption]. Effort includes content and imagery. Scores are editorial estimates out of 10 in taste / distinctness / feasibility order.

### 1. Before the Shelf

Give a supermarket buyer evidence for a ranging decision. **Visitor/job** · Category buyer shortlisting a SKU. **393px** · One pack at eye level with “View trade spec” below. **Interaction** · Pin two SKUs and print a comparison with dated field sources. **Look** · Ink, ivory, IBM Plex Mono for specs. **Imagery** · Real approved packs, generated shelf lighting. **Risk** · Confirm case count, GTIN and dimensions. Show missing fields as “Ask sales.” **Effort** M · **Scores** 9/9/8.

### 2. Prep for Service

Turn tomorrow’s covers into a case estimate. **Visitor/job** · Chef or caterer sizing an enquiry. **393px** · One stainless prep pan in raking light, with “Calculate cases” below. **Interaction** · Enter covers and grams per cover, then round up by case weight. HPB lists New Moon Singapore Laksa Paste at 24 × 100 g. [verified: https://www.hpb.gov.sg/schools/school-programmes/healthy-meals-suppliers-list/goh-joo-hin-pte-ltd/] **Look** · Charcoal, amber, Barlow. **Imagery** · Generated prep scene, approved real pack. **Risk** · Confirm case size with sales and have the chef set portions. **Effort** M · **Scores** 9/9/9.

### 3. Read the Pack

Find the exact product behind the label in your hand. **Visitor/job** · Shopper checking origin or allergens. **393px** · One oversized tin above an EN/中文 search field. **Interaction** · Flip between approved front and back photographs with a text transcript and official SKU link. One New Moon listing already supplies both views. [verified: https://www.gjheshop.com/product/new-moon-premium-wild-caught-new-zealand-abalone-425g/] **Look** · Midnight, ivory, Atkinson Hyperlegible with system CJK. **Imagery** · Real matching packs, generated matte set. **Risk** · Similar tins and packaging revisions. **Effort** M · **Scores** 9/8/9.

### 4. Find the Right Help

Route a purchase question to the right contact. **Visitor/job** · Shopper finding order or product support. **393px** · One sidelit carton above “Where did you buy it?” **Interaction** · Branch by purchase channel to eShop contact, original seller or corporate enquiry. **Look** · Graphite, cyan, Inter. **Imagery** · Generated unbranded carton. Real approved GJH mark optional. **Risk** · Confirm model-specific service and warranty routes before listing them. **Effort** S · **Scores** 8/8/10.

### 5. Rochor Road, On Record

Read the records behind the origin story. **Visitor/job** · Heritage-curious local comparing accounts. **393px** · One archival street crop above “Read the accounts.” **Interaction** · Keep the image fixed while switching between the company account and the attributed family interview. [verified: docs/gjh-story/01-origins.md] **Look** · Sepia, ink, Baskervville. **Imagery** · Rights-cleared period image for context or a labelled generated streetscape. **Risk** · Preserve unresolved chronology and avoid depicting an exact storefront. **Effort** M · **Scores** 9/9/8.

### 6. Inside the Working Day

Show what a role involves before a candidate applies. **Visitor/job** · Candidate or recruiter checking duties. **393px** · One staff portrait with “Explore the role” below. **Interaction** · Open published duties by task and follow the official application route. The careers page describes customer-service duties. [verified: https://www.gjhglobal.com/our-people/] **Look** · Slate, cream, Source Sans 3. **Imagery** · Consented real staff or generated empty workspace. **Risk** · Confirm vacancies and omit placeholder listings. **Effort** S · **Scores** 8/8/9.

### 7. Route to Market

Let a brand owner assess GJH’s distribution experience. **Visitor/job** · Brand principal checking category fit before contacting sales. **393px** · One loading-bay portrait with “Check category experience.” **Interaction** · Pick a sector, then inspect dated evidence by territory and relationship type. **Look** · Deep navy, signal orange, Archivo. **Imagery** · Real cleared facility photo or labelled generic loading bay. **Risk** · Confirm current capability and scope directly with GJH. **Effort** M · **Scores** 9/9/7.

## Top three

1. **Prep for Service** · A one-SKU pilot gives a chef a usable quantity before enquiry.
2. **Read the Pack** · Existing front/back assets support a concrete bilingual lookup test.
3. **Before the Shelf** · A printable comparison supports the buyer’s internal handoff once trade fields are confirmed.
