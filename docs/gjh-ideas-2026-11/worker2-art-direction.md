# GJH landing concepts · Art direction

Goh Joo Hin traces its beginnings to a provision store on Rochor Road in the 1940s [verified: https://gjhglobal.com/about-us/]. The 17-name research portfolio combines corporate and eShop listings after alias normalization [verified: docs/gjh-multibrand-directory.md]. Visitor needs, creative treatments, effort and scores are [assumption].

## What to borrow from the benchmarks

- **Give one object the frame.** Lusion’s *Oryzo AI* (2026 Awwwards Site of the Day) builds a launch around a cork coaster. Cartier’s 2025 winner gives timepieces distinct settings, while Apple’s AirPods page changes the view to explain the object. Keep framing consistent across states and change the crop when it answers a visitor’s question. [Oryzo award](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/oryzo-ai) · [Lusion case study](https://lusion.co/projects/oryzo_ai/) · [Cartier award](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025) · [Apple Singapore](https://www.apple.com/sg/airpods-pro/)
- **Use place and material to set the tone.** Terminal Industries (CSS Design Awards, 2025) puts its shipping yard at the centre of the corporate pitch. Aesop translates a gabion into a Singapore store façade. MUJI foregrounds materials and Kinfolk’s *Mujo Objects* studies sculptural forms and tactile surfaces. Apply that restraint with one light direction, one legible surface and space around the subject. [Terminal Industries](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/terminal-industries/47847/) · [Aesop Singapore](https://www.aesop.com.sg/pages/stores) · [MUJI](https://www.muji.com/sg/about/) · [Kinfolk](https://www.kinfolk.com/stories/mujo-objects/)
- **Choose a specific Singapore reference per scene.** Use the five-foot way for depth, Chin Mee Chin’s green floor tiles for glaze, and Peranakan relief for geometry. Draw print cues from a 1960s coffee bag, a 1970s Tiger Balm sign and a 1987 Philips newspaper ad. For the painted route, take Nanyang art’s tropical subject matter and flat colour planes as compositional cues. [NHB shophouses](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/singapore-shophouses/story) · [NHB kopitiam](https://www.roots.gov.sg/han/Neighborhoods/Katong-Joo-Chiat/HAN-Resources-Folder/Landmarks-of-Katong-Joo-Chiat) · [Coffee bag](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/hawker-culture) · [Trade sign](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/the-singapore-story-through-60-objects/self-government-and-independence/tiger-balm/story) · [1987 ad](https://www.straitstimes.com/multimedia/graphics/assets/pages/st180-microsite-2025/14-07/telling-the-singapore-story.pdf) · [National Gallery Singapore](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/learn-about-art/our-publications/digital-catalog/siapa-nama-kamu-exhibition-catalogue/chapters-overview/nanyang-reverie.html)

## Seven candidates

V22 uses one dominant image per chapter [verified: docs/gjh-v22/design-brief.md]. At 393 × 844, adapt that hierarchy with newly commissioned art, live HTML copy and visible 44 px actions inside the safe area. Use portrait WebP, local free font subsets, native links or controls, and optional vanilla JS with a readable static fallback and reduced-motion handling. Scores are taste / distinctness / feasibility, each out of 10. Effort includes imagery.

### 1. Five-Foot Way

*Cross a shophouse threshold to read the documented origins.*

**Visitor** Heritage reader seeking the source behind the origin story. **First screen** Full-bleed arcade, one bright doorway centred at eye level, title and “Step inside” low on the dark near wall. **Interaction** One tap replaces the exterior with a matched interior still and opens a source caption. **Look** Charcoal `#171A16`, glaze green `#426254`, warm ivory `#F1E6CC`. Cormorant Garamond + Instrument Sans. **Art** Generate two empty, unlettered scenes with matched light. Use a cleared GJH archive photograph only in the evidence view. **Risk** Label the imagined shop visibly and preserve the two origin accounts in the caption [verified: docs/gjh-story/README.md]. **M · 9/9/8.**

### 2. The Receiving Bay

*Treat the loading bay as the front door for trade enquiries.*

**Visitor** Retail buyer seeking the relevant enquiry route. **First screen** A single loading-bay opening under hard morning light, title on the dark jamb and plain-text sector choices near the thumb. **Interaction** Selecting a sector opens a short buyer checklist and GJH’s corporate sales link [verified: docs/gjh-v22/design-brief.md]. **Look** Asphalt `#171B1F`, chalk `#F4F1E8`, amber `#F1B84B`. Archivo Narrow + Inter. **Art** Generate one unmarked bay with a lone pallet silhouette. Use cleared GJH photography for any identified facility. **Risk** Illustrative scale could imply real capacity. Caption the scene as conceptual. **M · 9/9/8.**

### 3. Ink on Tin

*Read a package through the lens of Singapore trade print.*

**Visitor** Shopper identifying the exact pack before opening its product page. **First screen** One oversized crop of the New Moon 425 g abalone tin [verified: docs/gjh-v22/design-brief.md] under raking light, “Read the label” in the cream lower quarter. **Interaction** Front and rim buttons swap registered macro crops and expose a readable label transcription. **Look** Carbon `#211B17`, cinnabar `#A53127`, stock `#F4E8CB`. Barlow Condensed + Source Sans 3. **Art** Use a cleared real tin photograph. Generate only an unlettered two-ink paper surround. **Risk** Period styling may suggest a vintage edition. Caption the actual pack and keep the label colours accurate. **M · 9/8/8.**

### 4. Tropical Still Life

*Find pantry products through a series of painted ingredients.*

**Visitor** Pantry shopper looking for a packaged format of a chosen ingredient. **First screen** One cut pineapple in opaque gouache, tilted against indigo, with the heading and ingredient chooser on a light paper margin. **Interaction** Ingredient buttons swap the painting and reveal sourced format matches. **Look** Indigo `#1F3350`, pineapple `#E4B74D`, paper `#F5ECD8`. Fraunces + DM Sans. **Art** Generate original single-subject paintings. Use cleared pack photos only after selection. **Risk** Fresh-produce imagery may mislead. Name the verified canned or packaged format beside each match. **M · 8/9/9.**

### 5. Glaze Study

*Turn one glazed tile into the cover of a searchable directory.*

**Visitor** Someone who remembers a brand name and needs its official destination. **First screen** An enlarged tile corner with one hard reflection, above an ivory search panel visible without scrolling. **Interaction** Typeahead matches documented aliases in plain text. **Look** Ink `#182B26`, celadon `#779889`, ivory `#F6F0E2`. News Cycle + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. **Art** Generate one original relief pattern under grazing light. No GJH photograph needed. **Risk** Ornamental repetition dilutes focus. Keep the single cropped motif and give search clear contrast. **S · 8/8/10.**

### 6. Light Test

*Identify electronics by form in a single controlled studio setup.*

**Visitor** Electronics shopper trying to find the relevant brand page by device type. **First screen** One large silhouette with cobalt rim light, title and device selector in the black lower quarter. **Interaction** A labelled “Reveal details” button swaps outline for a matched lit still with the brand link. **Look** Graphite `#101219`, frost `#EDF2FF`, cobalt `#718BFF`. Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono. **Art** Generate the empty set. Use cleared manufacturer images for device geometry and marks. **Risk** Model recognition may imply availability. Link to the verified range and omit stock language. **L · 8/7/7.**

### 7. Cold Counter

*Use condensation as the curtain for GOGO’s drinks and ice cream [verified: https://gjhglobal.com/gogo/].*

**Visitor** Snack shopper looking for a drink or potong ice cream. **First screen** One frosted pane with amber light streaking across it, dark teal footer holding “Drinks” and “Potong” buttons. **Interaction** Tap either format to replace the pane with one clear product view and show the official GOGO page link. **Look** Teal `#103B3C`, ice `#E9F5EC`, amber `#F3AA47`. Archivo Black + Barlow. **Art** Generate unmarked ribbed glass with backlit condensation. Use a cleared GOGO photograph for each reveal. **Risk** Avoid implying store stock. **M · 8/9/8.**

## Top three

1. **Five-Foot Way** gives the strongest Singapore-specific opening and makes the source part of the reveal.
2. **The Receiving Bay** turns one architectural image into a practical starting point for a buyer.
3. **Tropical Still Life** offers a distinctive illustrated option whose first screen can be prototyped while pack photography is being cleared.
