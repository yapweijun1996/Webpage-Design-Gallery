# Shared brief — GJH (Goh Joo Hin, Singapore) webpage idea research
You are a research worker for a webpage design manager. Goal: deep research to propose NEW landing-page / website concept ideas for Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd (GJH), Singapore (multi-brand food, electronics, health distributor; 17 brands incl. New Moon, MILI, GOGO, Borsch Med, Sony, LG, Casio, Gigaset...).
Read first (repo evidence): docs/gjh-landing-roadmap.md, docs/gjh-landing-image-brief.md, docs/gjh-story/README.md, docs/gjh-multibrand-directory.md, docs/gjh-v22/design-brief.md, docs/gjh-v11-mobile-redesign-gap-report.md. Existing demos live in demos/corporate/gjh/ (v1..v25). Do NOT edit anything outside your output file.
Constraints / owner taste (from standing feedback):
- Owner tests on iPhone 393x844. Mobile-first.
- Approved bar: v22 (dark, cinematic, ONE dominant image per screen). Rejected as "no taste": collage-style mockups reusing the shared 98-image library (v20,v21,v23,v24,v25). New ideas must be distinct from the rejected ones, not necessarily from v22.
- Use only verifiable facts about GJH; mark every claim as [verified: source URL/file] or [assumption]. No invented awards, numbers or testimonials.
- Static, no-npm Python-generated gallery; ideas must be buildable as a self-contained HTML/CSS/JS demo.
Use web search for current external benchmarks (cite URLs). 
Deliverable: ONE markdown file (path given below), English, max ~1200 words, containing 6-8 distinct concept candidates. For each: name, one-line pitch, target visitor + their job-to-be-done, first-screen composition (mobile 393px), signature interaction, palette/typography direction, key imagery needed (what must be generated vs. real GJH photos), risks, effort S/M/L, and a 1-10 score for taste/distinctness/feasibility. End with your top-3 ranking and why.
