# GJH V11 stage 3: expanded shell and circulation

Date: 15 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore)

Status: **implemented as an opt-in, reversible ground-floor preview**. This report preserves its stage 3 snapshot; the subsequent [stage 4 atrium](gjh-v11-stage4-atrium.md) replaces the provisional seating and adds planting in the preview. The original three-store scene remains the default. In its directory, choose **Preview the expanded mall**, or open `demos/corporate/gjh/v11/?layout=expanded`; the preview directory links back to the original scene. Switching layouts navigates to a new page and preserves the current tab's demo bag when session storage is available. Neither layout is a real merchant, ordering system or building specification.

## Geometry and scope

The preview builds a 70 × 60 m floor, perimeter walls and ceiling, closed entrance glazing, a rectangular circulation loop, and 17 spatial slots. New Moon, MILI and GOGO remain the existing functioning rooms in their existing order, with their products, doors, map destinations, bag and directory. The other 14 slots are neutral, closed, numbered in the map and marked “COMING SOON” in 3D. They have no store navigation or claimed tenants. Their temporary opaque front panels are placeholders, not finished storefront design. The two north corners are reserved shell space. The south entrance is a visibly closed boundary and initial arrival point, not an exterior or exit simulation.

[expanded-layout.js](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/expanded-layout.js) records the 17 nominal bays, boundary, arrival and loop. It uses the [stage 1 plan](references/gjh-mall-spatial-plan.json) with a rigid translation: `worldX = planX - 25`, `worldZ = 48 - planY`. This keeps the first three existing stores at their original positions. The map and placeholder geometry read the same expanded records; the existing stores retain their validated [spatial registry](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/spatial.js) and journeys. The first three interiors still have their original 10.5 m nominal depth inside 12 m planning bays; they are not newly rebuilt 12 m-deep shops. The store list continues to describe the three available stores only.

The loop centerline runs through `(-12, 3)`, `(32, 3)`, `(32, 39)` and `(-12, 39)` in world metres. The planned perimeter corridors are nominally 6 m wide. Existing outer courtyard walls are omitted in this preview, and the old central upright ring, water feature and matching collider are removed. The old west terrace and courtyard plants that occupied circulation are omitted; the service desk and showcase, their collisions, interaction anchors and showcase products move together into the interior. The directory kiosk keeps its interaction but uses a neutral screen instead of the outdated three-room diagram. Existing seating and the current experimental lift are retained temporarily; the lift has **not** yet been moved into the stage 1 shaft. The original scene retains its previous fixtures. Stage 4 still owns the permanent atrium/seating design, stage 5 the complete store migration, stages 6–8 storefront content, lift/facilities, and material/lighting refinement.

## Verification

[Expanded browser test](../tests/browser_gjh_expanded_shell.py) runs the actual 3D world and Rapier collision controller. It covers keyboard input at 320 × 700, 390 × 844, 768 × 1024, 1440 × 900 and 844 × 390; trusted emulated touch input at 390 × 844; map positions and all three existing physical door approaches; directory, products and bag; and the legacy V1 visitor at 390 × 844. At 1440 × 900 it walks the loop clockwise and pushes a cart counterclockwise without teleporting between waypoints, checking arrival, floor height, and camera visibility. The loop was driven by bounded simulation steps, while keyboard and touch were separately exercised through browser input. This validates the tested centerline and cart envelope, not every point across a 6 m width. It also tests switching layouts with a retained bag, actual `WEBGL_lose_context` loss and recovery, and no-JavaScript and failed-physics directory fallback. [Test output](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/expanded-browser.log).

[Layout contract test](../tests/gjh_expanded_layout.test.mjs) compares all 17 runtime slot bounds and numbering to the stage 1 JSON under the stated translation and checks the three existing store identities and map projection. Regression checks passed in isolated copies to protect existing screenshot evidence: [generator check](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/generator-check.log), [gallery unit tests](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/gallery-unit.log), [gallery browser](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/gallery-browser.log), [GJH demo browser](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/demos-browser.log), [PWA](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/pwa-browser.log), [existing spatial/browser journeys](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/spatial-browser.log), [mode lifecycle](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/modes-browser.log), and [hybrid directory/bag](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/hybrid-browser.log). The demo browser suite had no axe bundle supplied; its reported zero violations are not an accessibility audit. Initial demo and PWA runs could not write their results because the isolated copy omitted the evidence directory; after creating it, both passed on the final source snapshot. Earlier default-scene regression runs preceded the final expanded-only kiosk screen change; the expanded test and generator, demo and PWA checks used the final source.

## Local performance observations

The [comparison procedure](../tests/browser_gjh_expanded_performance.py) samples the original and expanded scene at the same original courtyard arrival and direction, DPR 2, Auto quality, local HTTP, headless Chrome 157 with ANGLE Metal on an Apple M4. It records renderer counters and 120 `requestAnimationFrame` intervals; these are scheduling observations, not GPU frame times or a device performance guarantee. Each value is a single local run, not a statistical benchmark. [Raw observations](evidence/gjh-stage3-20261115/performance.log).

| 390 × 844 | Original | 158 / 821,750 | 181 / 832,386 | 16.7 / 16.8 |
| 390 × 844 | Expanded | 149 / 789,086 | 172 / 799,722 | 16.7 / 16.7 |
| 1440 × 900 | Original | 196 / 824,762 | 219 / 835,398 | 16.7 / 16.8 |
| 1440 × 900 | Expanded | 185 / 790,092 | 208 / 800,728 | 16.7 / 16.7 |

The existing 200-draw-call laboratory limit in `browser_gjh_budget.py` covers its specified non-cart scenes, not all cart views. The matched desktop expanded cart exceeds 200, as does the original cart; this remains a performance concern for later optimisation and is not hidden by changing the threshold. The separate loop sample reached 210 calls with the cart. Values depend on camera and quality tier.

## Remaining limits

No physical phone, tablet, gamepad, Safari, screen reader, outdoor access, full-width sweep of every walkway, all store/cart paths, or field performance has been validated. This slice is not a finished interior design: reserve spaces and closed bays lack tenant-specific content, seating and the lift remain provisional, the larger upper floor is not built, and seasonal templates and decorations are later work. Proposed dimensions are virtual planning assumptions and do not establish real-world code compliance.
