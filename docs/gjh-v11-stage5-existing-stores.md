# GJH V11 stage 5: existing store integration

Date: 15 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore)

Status: **implemented in the expanded ground-floor layout, now the default**. The original three-store scene is available with `?layout=classic`; `?layout=expanded` selects the default layout explicitly. This slice incorporates the [stage 3 shell](gjh-v11-stage3-expanded-shell.md) and [stage 4 atrium](gjh-v11-stage4-atrium.md), and retains the standalone V11 PWA added to upstream main after stage 2. Application version is 1.1.48; its build ID and both workers are generated from current sources.

## Storefront and navigation

New Moon, MILI and GOGO remain S01, S02 and S03, with their existing stable IDs, ordered positions, room layouts, artwork, glass sliding doors and interactions. In the expanded scene their opaque facade panels are replaced by transparent, collision-bearing storefront windows with warm stone, wood, green and restrained brass framing. The actual existing interiors are visible through the windows. The doors keep the original nominal 4.7 m opening and physics; solid glass prevents walking through the display window. No new brand claims, fictional products or external assets were added. The original scene retains its existing facade.

Each directory story now offers its own **Visit … in 3D** action. It uses the same validated store ID and arrival as the map, is available only after 3D is ready, and is hidden in no-JavaScript and graphics/physics failure modes. New Moon product-card links continue to use the same path. Location classification in the expanded scene now bounds the existing store lookup using its shared spatial records instead of separate hard-coded edges, so the nearby planned bays do not incorrectly report themselves as an open store. Existing old IDs and original-scene zone boundary behavior remain unchanged.

The three current rooms are still approximately 10.5 m deep inside the 12 m-deep gross stage 1 bays. The remaining strip is enclosed and not presented as occupied retail space or an operational back room. This is a functional and facade integration, not a claim that the rooms have been physically extended, or that the other 14 planned bays are open.

New Moon retains its four existing SKU IDs, official packshot references, detailed unofficial 3D-model disclosure, product viewer and demo bag. MILI and GOGO retain their original brand cover artwork and explicit lack of verified product packshots; no inventory, prices, checkout or real order is claimed. Store navigation and views preserve the current tab's bag.

## Local performance and limits

The [matched local performance sample](evidence/gjh-stage5-20261115/performance.log), Chrome 157 / ANGLE Metal on Apple M4, DPR 2, Auto quality at the existing courtyard arrival, measured 151 idle / 174 cart draw calls at 390 × 844 and 184 idle / 207 cart at 1440 × 900. In-store no-cart samples in the focused browser log were 110/90/73 calls at 390 and 124/138/120 at 1440 for New Moon/MILI/GOGO. These are individual camera-dependent observations, not worst-case or GPU-time guarantees. The desktop cart still exceeds the existing 200-call non-cart laboratory reference, as in stages 3–4; that threshold was not changed.

The other 14 spaces remain closed, neutral placeholders. Seasonal packs, real operations, a final lift and upper floor, complete accessibility certification and physical-device/Safari testing remain outside this slice. Browser emulation and one machine's renderer statistics do not establish field performance. The official eShop links were checked as URLs and were not used to place orders.
