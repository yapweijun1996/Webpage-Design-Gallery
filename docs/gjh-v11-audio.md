# GJH v11 sound and music

Open **Tools** for **Mute sound**, **Background music**, and the three bus sliders under **Volume settings**. The speaker button and **M** share the master mute. The **Music on** and **Music off** status is announced when playback changes. The Sony and Casio **Demo audio** controls share one shop mute. It applies to showroom action cues and shop motifs.

Arrival gestures and Enter now can construct a suspended audio context during entry. After the loader hides, the first trusted scene gesture resumes the context. Deep links can prepare it during stationary idle time, with a stationary non-movement gesture as a fallback. On Low phones a saved music opt-in can prepare the context during entry. Otherwise the first footsteps and effects are silent until the visitor opens Tools after a sound request or explicitly enables sound. Background music waits at least 1.5 seconds after audio resumes. The default bus levels are 26% music, 65% effects and 30% ambience. Background music starts enabled except on Low phones, with reduced motion, or when the browser exposes Data Saver as enabled. Selecting Background music explicitly overrides those defaults. Master mute and a hidden tab suspend the audio context and cancel current speech. Returning to the 3D scene restores eligible playback. Settings persist under `gjh-v11-audio-v1` in local storage and fall back to page memory if storage is blocked. [Web Audio context lifecycle](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/) · [Chrome autoplay and Web Audio](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/autoplay#web_audio)

## Score and cues

- One audio-clock timeline plays eight bars at 72 BPM. The score uses major-seventh and ninth voicings, soft electric piano, plucked bass and a swung brush on the offbeats. The courtyard uses the warm C-major voicing, the upper deck shifts up to D and uses a mallet timbre, and shops can change the key and timbre at a bar boundary. Each shop has a short entry motif on that same timeline.
- The lookahead wakes every 75 ms and schedules up to 180 ms ahead. The loop wraps without restarting its clock or downloading audio. A visible tram caption or its narration lowers the music bus to 32% of its selected level. A played shop effect briefly ducks the same bus.
- Footsteps follow collision-corrected distance on foot and stop while seated or riding. Store and lift doors cue at motion boundaries. The lift has an activation ding and a two-note floor arrival signal. Successful pickup, bag additions and drop or return each have a tick.
- The shop effect table follows the runtime actions derived from the interior program. Tea, drinks and watering use a pour patch. Trays and samples use a clink or short tick. Other patches cover bells, paper, cases, drawers, controls, the Sony sample, Casio keys and Gigaset ring. Cancelling a showroom action releases its voice. The mute action uses its existing visual and text feedback.
- The tram plays a departure bell, a quiet hum while moving and a station chime. **Voice** is off on a fresh visit. The visible brand caption and optional speech share English, Simplified Chinese or Malay copy. Skip, End and Pause cancel current speech. Resume reads the current caption from the beginning. The Voice toggle checks for a compatible system voice. A saved Voice preference can narrate with the browser's default voice before the toggle is used in the current visit. Captions remain available with speech or sound disabled.

## Runtime and verification

`assets/audio.js` owns the context, mixer buses, oscillator pool and preferences. The entry, player and world bundles reference the same external module, as do the lazy shop and lift modules. Phone mode has four music slots and two shared effect/ambience slots. Foreground cues can reclaim a footstep or rail slot. Wave coefficients can be prepared before the first gesture. The context graph is prepared one operation at a time in idle frames with 1.5 ms of available time. Effects voices are created on demand and the remaining slots are prewarmed in idle frames. The music bus and its four voices are prepared only when background music is enabled, before the first scheduler tick. `__mall.performanceReport()` exposes `audioCreateMs`, `audioCreateDuring` (`loader`, `idle` or `gesture`) and `audioCreateFrame` (0 before the scene begins rendering). `audioInitMs` includes construction and the synchronous part of the first resume call. `audioInitSliceMaxMs` tracks the largest graph setup slice. Disabling music disconnects its bus and clears the timer. Playback updates are driven by scene events, travelled distance and the audio clock.
