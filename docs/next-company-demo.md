## Why this company follows GJH

On 17 November the main-branch catalogue contained one company and eight designs (V1 and V5–V11). The review also covered five local landing-page studies (V12–V16). GJH already gives a visitor several ways to discover a company, browse brands, inspect products and explore a virtual mall. At Thow Kwang the visitor has to decide **how to experience the place**. Someone visiting alone can explore independently or choose a public class. A teacher can find a guided group visit or a hands-on workshop. Each path has an official destination that a one-page demo can help the visitor find. ## First-page brief

1. **Introduce the place.** Open on an original side-view illustration of the long brick kiln. A short caption explains why wood firing gives each piece a different surface. A visitor ready to go can jump directly to “Plan a visit”. [NHB account of the craft](https://www.roots.gov.sg/ich-landing/ich/making-of-wood-fired-pottery)
2. **Make the kiln readable.** Offer three tappable details for the firebox, the chambers where pots are stacked, and the glaze left by wood ash. Give each a visible text caption that also works with a keyboard and with the illustration switched off. Label the drawing as a schematic. [NHB profile](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/Thow-Kwang-Pottery-Jungle-Stoking-the-fires-of-Singapores-dragon-kiln/Thow-Kwang-Pottery-Jungle-Stoking-the-fires-of-Singapores-dragon-kiln)
3. **Help the visitor choose.** “Visiting on my own” offers a self-guided visit or public workshop. “Bringing a group” leads to a guided kiln tour or a private workshop. Let the operator show available dates, prices and group conditions. [Visit and tour](https://www.potteryjungle.com/dragonkiln-tour) · [Public workshops](https://www.potteryjungle.com/pottery-workshop) · [Private workshops](https://www.potteryjungle.com/private-workshops)

**First-version acceptance.** On a phone, a first-time visitor can identify where the firing starts and open the correct official page for either a solo outing or a group visit. The same tasks work with the keyboard through text links. Use original illustration and independent-demo attribution. A prospective build needs neither live availability nor a local booking form.
