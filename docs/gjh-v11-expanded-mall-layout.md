# GJH V11 expanded mall: proposed ground-floor layout

Date: 15 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore)  
Status: **historical rough draft**. The owner liked the direction; [stage 1 spatial plan](gjh-v11-stage1-spatial-plan.md) and its [updated drawing](references/gjh-mall-floorplan-stage1.png) supersede the rough spatial allocation below. Both are conceptual and await owner review; neither is implemented, measured from a real building or approved for construction.

## Brief and reference

The owner requested a dimensioned 2D layout and chose an expanded mall with separate candidate storefronts after asking for research into the broader Goh Joo Hin brand portfolio. The [earlier dimensioned plan](references/gjh-mall-floorplan-draft.png) is preserved as a historical draft; it is generated by [the drawing source](references/gjh-mall-floorplan-draft.py) using Pillow. It is a conceptual allocation, not a measured survey, a code-compliant building plan or an indication that a brand has agreed to participate. Frontage openings, precise doors, back-of-house access, lifts and facilities need detailed design.

The established visual direction is a warm modern boutique mall: stone, glass, wood, restrained green and gold, grouped seating, low planting, and a flexible seasonal area. Remove the existing upright gold ring. Preserve the current left-to-right order of New Moon, MILI and GOGO within the north row, while expanding beyond the current three stores. See the [interior redesign brief](gjh-v11-modern-interior-redesign.md).

## Historical rough geometry (superseded by stage 1)

The current scene is a fictional metre-scale model, not a physical GJH building. In the present source, the main floor is 30.4 m × 29.4 m, centered at z=3.5, with three stores arranged at x=-10, 0 and 10. The store backs are near z=-10.5 and the concourse's rear wall is near z=18. The current V2 lift is near (x=0, z=16), and a separate upper slab measures 12 m × 10 m. See [world.js](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/world.js) and [elevator-v2.js](../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/elevator-v2.js). These are source-code observations, not a verified physical property or a fresh survey of all colliders.

The earlier diagram is a **new, hypothetical 70 m wide × 60 m deep ground-floor shell**, compared with the existing ~30.4 m × 29.4 m main floor. Its dimensions are planning assumptions chosen to give the requested 17 independent candidate stores and common space a legible first layout. They are not existing site dimensions, an estimate of real mall requirements, construction approval or a commitment to build at this size. Storefronts face the atrium; the entry is at the bottom of the diagram. The earlier draft did not define an upper-floor connection; the later stage 1 proposal now describes both landings.

| Proposed allocation | Calculation | Area |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Ground-floor shell | 70 × 60 m | 4,200 m² |
| Five north stores | 5 × (10 × 12 m) | 600 m² |
| Twelve west/east stores | 12 × (10 × 8 m) | 960 m² |

| **Total** | 600 + 960 + 240 + 2,400 | **4,200 m²** |

Areas are gross diagram blocks; walls, storefront thresholds, service rooms, accessibility clearances, structure and building systems are not deducted. Store modules are proposed equal blocks rather than asserted usable retail areas. Within the common area the draft places an 8 × 8 m lift zone, an 8 × 8 m restroom/facilities zone, two 10 × 8 m lounge zones, a 14 × 5 m low planting zone, a 20 × 9 m flexible event zone, and two 10 × 6 m directory/guest-service zones. These all sit inside the 2,400 m² common area and must not be added to it. Reserve blocks currently have no resolved service access and are placeholders only.

The gold dashed lines indicate **suggested visitor movement**, not measured clear-width boundaries or fire escape routes. Plan the exact door widths, sightlines, turning circles, seasonal-display boundaries, cart routes, facilities, accessible paths and emergency egress before implementation; no safety or regulatory compliance is claimed. Avoid inventing a second full retail floor: the current upper level is a small prototype, and this draft covers the ground floor only.

## Theme strategy

The central event block can host temporary Christmas trees and gifts, Lunar New Year lanterns and flowers, Diwali lighting or an everyday installation. Theme changes should use one coordinated selection for color, lighting and removable props while keeping the fixed shell, entrances, brands and circulation understandable. Real decoration geometry, lighting, switching behavior, accessible setup and safe removal remain unimplemented. Review seasonal props against actual path clearance; never bring back the upright gold ring.
