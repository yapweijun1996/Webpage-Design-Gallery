CHAPTERS / FOLLOW THE ORDER

A request is not a packing instruction.

Each stage answers a different question: what did the customer ask for, is there enough information to review it, and has the merchant approved it?

01 / THE ORDER

Keep what the customer actually said.

The request names the product and quantity, but not the delivery method. Recording what is known does not mean guessing what is missing or committing to fulfil the order.

02 / REVIEW STEP

Complete the details before review.

Confirm the delivery method before the merchant starts reviewing PS-0124. A complete record makes review possible, but it is not approval by itself.

03 / PACKING STEP

Make approval mean something.

PS-0124 becomes ready to pack only after the merchant explicitly approves it in the simulation. The reviewed details stay on the record so the packing team knows what was approved.

OUT–09 / INTENDED OUTCOME

Clear details, clear decision, clear handoff.

What this example demonstrates

  • A consistent order reference through the stages
  • A delivery method confirmed before review
  • Explicit simulated approval before packing

What it does not claim

  • No real customer endorsement or results
  • No measured time savings or sales uplift
  • No actual payment, stock reservation or shipment

PS-0124 and all scenario details are simulated for this clickable design preview. No interaction on this page creates or approves a real order.
