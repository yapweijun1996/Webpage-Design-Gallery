SVC-05 / AI INTEGRATION

AI drafts. People decide.

Prepare responses to repetitive questions, then have a person check the facts, edit the draft and approve the final reply.

Follow the demo caseSee the workflow

FLOW-02 / ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW

Draft, review, and handle what’s missing.

Example question: “Can I use an SPL2 wire connector for this project?”

01 / AI draft

“Could you share the wire size and the application so we can check suitability?” A draft can ask a better question without guessing at a spec.

02 / Human review

Check the verified specifications and the buyer’s requirements. Edit unclear wording and approve only a reply you are prepared to stand behind.

03 / Handoff when details are missing

If the catalogue is unclear or important details are missing, route the enquiry to someone who can resolve it. Do not invent a specification, stock level or delivery date.

USE-01 / STARTING POINTS

Where an assistant can help.

Start with work that is repetitive, easy to check and reversible.

Catalogue questions

Draft a reply using verified product information; pass technical uncertainty to a person.

Enquiry summaries

Summarise the requested item, quantity and open questions for a reviewer.

Follow-up drafts

Suggest a short request for missing information. The reviewer still decides what to send.

Illustrative example only. The preview does not generate a response, access customer data or send messages.
