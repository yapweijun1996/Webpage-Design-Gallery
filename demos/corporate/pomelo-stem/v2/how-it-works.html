WKF–02 / THE HUMAN APPROVAL GATE

A clear request is not yet an approved order.

The merchant checks the order before it moves to packing. Automation can help organise information; the review step keeps the decision visible.

Check the details

Review the original request alongside the proposed order. Resolve missing or ambiguous information before continuing.

Confirm product and quantity

Check the item, variant and requested amount against the information available to the seller.

Approve to pack

Move a confirmed order to the next stage only after the required review. Keep unresolved requests in review.

WKF–03 / CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Know what each stage means.

  1. Enquiry received

    A customer expresses interest. The request still needs confirmation.

  2. Pending review

    The seller checks the details and clarifies anything incomplete.

  3. Ready to pack

    The reviewed order is available for packing. This does not mean it has shipped.

  4. Handed off

    The fulfilment handoff is recorded separately from review and packing.

This is an illustrative workflow, not a live order tracker. No payment, message or shipment is created by exploring this preview.
