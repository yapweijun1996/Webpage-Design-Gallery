SVC-04 / CUSTOMER ENQUIRIES

Start in WhatsApp.

Customers review, edit and send their own draft. The merchant checks the details before confirming any next step.

See the demo caseExplore Seller Studio

FLOW-01 / ENQUIRY HANDOFF

The enquiry flow.

A draft is a starting point, not a confirmed order.

  1. Customer edits and sends

    In a live flow, the customer can change the item, quantity or question and decides whether to send.

  2. Merchant reviews

    Check what the buyer needs and confirm any missing details directly with them.

  3. Ready to pack

    Only after the merchant accepts the order does it move to preparation.

CTRL-01 / HUMAN CONTROL

Keep enquiry and fulfilment separate.

This concept shows a human handoff, not WhatsApp Cloud automation.

The buyer owns the message

Drafts stay editable and are never sent in the background.

The merchant owns approval

“Received” and “accepted” are separate states. Stock, payment and delivery must be verified independently.

No real message, order, payment or shipping request is created in this preview.
