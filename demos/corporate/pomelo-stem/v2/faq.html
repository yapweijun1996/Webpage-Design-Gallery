Can I start with just WhatsApp?

Start with enquiries and manual review. You can explore WhatsApp Orders without committing to a storefront.

What does my team need to review?

Check details that affect the order, such as quantities, stock, pricing, delivery and any exceptions, before confirming with the customer.

Can I connect an existing workflow?

Start by mapping where an order enters and where it is checked. Compatibility and access depend on the tools you use. See Custom Systems.

What is shown in the demo?

The demo case uses sample data to show an order moving from enquiry to review and handoff. It does not place a real order.

Does AI confirm orders for me?

In this blueprint, AI helps organize information for review. Your team keeps the final say. See AI Integration.
