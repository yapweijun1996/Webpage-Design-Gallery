ILLUSTRATIVE ARCHITECTURE · SCOPE DEFINED TOGETHER

IMP–01 / IMPLEMENTATION PHASES

Map. Connect. Validate.

A practical starting point is one repeatable workflow, not a replacement for every system at once.

01 / MAP

Make the handoffs visible.

Identify the inputs, decisions, exceptions and people involved. Agree what a completed order actually means.

02 / CONNECT

Bring the right information together.

Define how order details, stock information and team responsibilities should connect, with clear boundaries around existing tools.

03 / VALIDATE

Check the complete journey.

Walk through representative orders and exceptions. Confirm that each person can understand the status and the next action.

SCOPE / BUILT AROUND YOUR OPERATIONS

Keep what works. Improve what slows you down.

Good starting points

  • Repeated copying between order records
  • Unclear review and approval ownership
  • Missing context between stock and fulfilment
  • Manual status updates across a team

What we define first

  • The workflow and its measurable acceptance criteria
  • Data sources and permitted access
  • Responsibilities, exceptions and approval boundaries
  • A focused first release and validation plan

Integration availability depends on the systems, access and scope agreed for your project. This preview does not imply an existing connection to your business data.
