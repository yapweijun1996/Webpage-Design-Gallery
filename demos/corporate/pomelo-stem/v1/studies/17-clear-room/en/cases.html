Three ways these services could work

These examples illustrate possible workflows. They are not client case studies or live integrations.

A possible story

Group enrolment without another spreadsheet

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    One organiser, several attendees

    A training provider receives seat requests from team leads. Names arrive later, while the lead still needs to know whether seats are held.

  2. 02

    Make the booking status the main event

    The brief starts with one clear status block: requested, held or confirmed. Attendee details sit below, apart from the decision.

  3. 03

    Keep seats and names in one record

    Custom Systems track group size, attendee edits and confirmation. Staff decide when a hold becomes a confirmed booking.
