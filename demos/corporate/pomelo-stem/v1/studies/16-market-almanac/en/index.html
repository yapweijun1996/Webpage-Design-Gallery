A possible story

A produce order that survives a changing harvest

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    The stall stocks by the morning, not the month

    A produce seller posts a weekly list, but the available greens change after the next delivery. Buyers differ on which substitutions they accept.

  2. 02

    Give the list a market-day edition

    A brochure-like page shows the current edition and places substitutions and collection details alongside the produce list.

  3. 03

    Check changes before filling the basket

    Online Shopping displays today’s items. WhatsApp Order records the buyer’s approved alternatives before staff pack.
