Growth Editorial / Cases

Three ways these services could work

These examples illustrate possible workflows. They are not client case studies or live integrations.

A possible story

A seed shop’s spring release

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    The season starts in a notebook

    A seed shop takes preorders in person, but customers outside town cannot see which herb and vegetable varieties are still available.

  2. 02

    Publish the growing guide

    The shop builds a seasonal Online Shopping collection and pairs each product with sowing notes and a suitable container size.

  3. 03

    Close the season without guesswork

    The shop marks sold-out varieties in the catalogue and sends late readers to the next seasonal collection.

Cases

More illustrative examples

  • 01

    Two ways to place an order

    Let customers start in the online store or on WhatsApp, while your team follows one order-review process.

    Browse → Order online or on WhatsApp → Team review
  • 02

    An AI-assisted product enquiry

    Use the product catalogue to answer common questions and hand off unusual requests to the team.

    Product question → Catalogue answer → Team support
  • 03

    Order handoff to fulfilment

    Give the operations team a clear view of the order, what stock to check and who should prepare it.

    Review order → Check stock → Prepare fulfilment

Start with one task your customers or team find difficult. Describe how you handle it today and what you would like to change.
