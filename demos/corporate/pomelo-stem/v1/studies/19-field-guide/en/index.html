Four ways to connect your business

Customers can shop online or reach you on WhatsApp. Bring the handover into the systems your team uses to review and fulfil orders.

  • 01

    Online Shopping

    Give customers a place to browse products and start an order. Checkout and payment options depend on the project.

  • 02

    WhatsApp Order

    Turn a product enquiry into an order summary your customer can confirm and your team can review.

  • 03

    AI Integration

    Connect AI to business data and everyday tools. Agree with your team where people need to confirm the next step.

  • 04

    Custom Systems

    Connect orders, stock and team tasks so staff can see what is ready and what needs attention.

A possible story

Help a first-time hiker choose what to bring

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    A wall of gear is hard to shop

    A new hiker knows the trip length, but not which gear matters or which quantities to choose. A long product list does not answer that.

  2. 02

    Let the reader follow a trail

    The guide orders choices by trip, weather and essentials. It shows which questions can be answered from the catalogue and when to ask staff.

  3. 03

    Build the basket in steps

    Online Shopping groups compatible essentials. AI Integration can answer catalogue questions and hand unfamiliar ones to the shop team.
