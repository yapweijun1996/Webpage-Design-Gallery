Field LabDesign 14

A possible story

A faster repair intake with a clear approval step

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    Good notes arrived in the wrong shape

    Customers describe equipment faults in long messages. A technician must pull out the model, symptoms and previous work before deciding what to inspect.

  2. 02

    Draw the approval line into the workflow

    A framed workbench separates evidence, a draft and technician review. The three stages never look like the same status.

  3. 03

    Draft the ticket, not the diagnosis

    AI Integration prepares an intake summary and marks missing fields. The technician corrects it and approves the service ticket.
