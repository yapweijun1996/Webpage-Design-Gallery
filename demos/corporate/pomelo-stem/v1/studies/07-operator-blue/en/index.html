A possible story

The repair shop’s waiting board

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study

  1. 01

    Promised dates outrun spare parts

    An appliance repair counter books jobs while technicians order parts separately, so the counter cannot tell which jobs are ready.

  2. 02

    Put blocked work on the board

    A Custom System distinguishes awaiting parts, ready for repair and ready for pickup, with a clear person responsible at each handoff.

  3. 03

    Make the next call from the same record

    The counter can check whether the needed part has arrived before inviting the customer to collect the appliance.
