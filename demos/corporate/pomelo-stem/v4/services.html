Where Pomelo STEM fits

You can start with one step. Choose the part of your workflow that needs the most attention.

One order, four places to improve

Online Shopping

Give customers a clear place to find the right product.

Where it helps
Before the message: give customers a product page they can use to check what they want to order.
What the team can do next
Example: a customer can find the SPL2 wire connector before asking whether they can order two.

WhatsApp Orders

Turn a product enquiry into details your team can review.

Where it helps
When the message arrives: record the item and quantity, and flag anything the customer still needs to confirm.
What the team can do next
Example: the team sees “SPL2 × 2” and can tell at a glance that the receiving method is still missing.

AI Integration

Help prepare routine drafts while keeping approval with your team.

Where it helps
Before approval: use a draft to organise the request. The merchant still checks the details and decides.
What the team can do next
Example: a draft keeps the unconfirmed detail visible for review instead of treating it as a fact.

Custom Systems

Connect the checked order to the team responsible for the next step.

Where it helps
After approval: show the packing team the checked order and what happens next.
What the team can do next
Example: after approval, the team sees “Ready to pack” and “Customer pickup” on the same record. The order has not shipped.
