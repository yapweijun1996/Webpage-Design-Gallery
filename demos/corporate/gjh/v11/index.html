How to explore

W A S D or arrow keys to walk.
Shift to run. Q / E to turn left / right. Drag the scene to look around.
F to inspect a nearby display.

On touch screens, drag anywhere on the open scene to move. Keep that finger down and drag with a second finger to look around. Lift to stop; tap Run to change pace. Buttons and panels keep their own actions.

Choose any store on the map to go to its entrance. Prefer a page? Choose List view above.
