On touch screens, drag anywhere on the open scene to move. Keep that finger down and drag with a second finger to look around. Lift to stop; tap Run to change pace. Buttons and panels keep their own actions. All eight benches support Sit / Stand. Shop benches face the entrance; approach the open seat side. Use Cart beside Help to start or stow a cart. Leave about 3 metres of clear floor around you to start pushing. You can also use Bag. While seated, walking is locked; use Stand or Escape to leave. A procedural sit/stand transition plays with walking locked. Actions use a close-up view, then restore your previous camera. Escape reverses entry; reduced-motion uses an immediate pose. The original visitor uses a procedural sitting pose and a generic carried bag.

Distances are approximate horizontal metres to fictional demo interaction points, not physical store measurements. The outline marks a whole shelf; other labels mark an interaction point.
