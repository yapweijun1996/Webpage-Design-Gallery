W A S D or arrow keys to walk.
Shift to run. Q / E to turn left / right. Drag the scene to look around.
F to interact with a nearby target.

Press V or use First person in Tools to switch views. Drag the scene or use the right stick to look around.

Use the mouse wheel, pinch on the trackpad or screen, or use + and − on the keyboard or in Tools to zoom. For touch zoom, start both fingers on the scene away from the joystick and move them together or apart.

On touch screens, drag the right joystick to move and drag the scene to look around. Turn on Full-screen joystick to move from anywhere and look with a second finger. Walking is the default. Tap Run to toggle running. Buttons and panels keep their own actions. All benches support Sit / Stand. Shop benches face the entrance; approach the open seat side. Use Shopping Cart in Tools to start or stow the demo push cart. Leave about 3 metres of clear floor around you to start pushing. Inventory Bag contains collected demo items, not purchases. Neither Bag nor Shopping Cart creates an order. While seated, walking is locked; use Stand or Escape to leave. A sit/stand transition plays with walking locked. The seat camera moves back to frame the visitor. Standing restores your previous view. Escape reverses entry; reduced-motion uses an immediate pose. The original visitor uses a procedural sitting pose and a generic carried bag.

Distances are approximate horizontal metres to fictional demo interaction points, not physical store measurements. The outline marks a whole shelf; other labels mark an interaction point.

Choose any store on the map to go to its entrance. Prefer a page? Choose Store Directory above.

    Expanded preview: lift and facilities

    The lift is in the north-west court of the expanded preview and serves a small upper viewing landing. The directory is west of the entrance, Guest services is east of the entrance, and the featured New Moon collection is beside the west court. The lift is visitor-only. Before riding, stow the cart and add any held product to your bag or return it to its original shelf. Call the lift when needed, then choose Ride after it arrives. The original visitor (v1) stays on the ground floor.

    Public restrooms are on the east ground-floor arcade. Men, Women and an all-user Accessible restroom have separate entrances. Guest services is self-service. The north service reserves are closed.
