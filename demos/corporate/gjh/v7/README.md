# Goh Joo Hin - Heritage Noir (V7)

An independent, unofficial design concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Goh Joo Hin. Brand logos and New Moon product imagery are official assets sourced from GJH, included with user-confirmed permission. Other photographs are AI-generated illustrations, not official photographs or product representations.

From the repository root run `python -m http.server 8000`, then open `http://localhost:8000/demos/corporate/gjh/v7/`. No build or backend is required. The contact links open the official GJH website.

Company information is sourced from GJH's [About Us](https://www.gjh.com.sg/about-us/) and [brand pages](https://www.gjh.com.sg/explore-our-brand/); see the [research profile](../research/company-profile.md). Verify current commercial relationships and usage rights before commercial publication.

Official New Moon assets: [product image](https://www.gjhglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/BN-250ml-Range-1024x683.jpg) and [logo](https://www.gjhglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/nm-brand.jpg). Permission to use these New Moon assets in the public demo was confirmed by the user.

Official additional logos: [MILI](https://www.gjhglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/mili-brand.jpg) and [GOGO](https://www.gjhglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/gogo-brand.jpg), used with user-confirmed permission.
