# Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd — Company Profile

> Research profile for an independent design concept. This document is not an official company publication and does not imply endorsement. Details are based on public sources reviewed on 11 November 2026 and should be confirmed with GJH before publication or business use.

## Overview

Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd (GJH) is a Singapore business whose website describes activities in fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), electronics, and health and nutrition. Its story began as a provision store on Rochor Road in the 1940s. Today, its website describes a regional network connecting brands and products with customers across Asia Pacific. [1]

## Business Areas

- **FMCG:** Food and beverage products, including the New Moon, MILI, and GOGO product lines. [2][3][4]
- **Electronics:** GJH's portfolio includes electronics brands. Its Sony page describes GJH as an appointed distributor for specified Sony product ranges; the territory, scope, and current status should be verified directly. [5]
- **Health and nutrition:** Borsch Med offers health supplement and personal care products. [6]

## Selected Brands

| Brand | Publicly described relationship and products |
| --- | --- |
| New Moon | A GJH-managed and distributed food product line; its history identifies abalone as the first product. [2][7] |
| MILI | A GJH-managed and distributed canned food product line, including fruit, vegetables, meat, seafood, and sauces. [3] |
| GOGO | A GJH-managed and distributed product line covering drinks and ice cream. [4] |
| Borsch Med | A healthcare supplement brand described on GJH's website. [6] |

## Selected History

- **1940s:** The Goh brothers began a provision store on Rochor Road. [1]
- **1959:** New Moon was established, according to its brand history. [7]
- **1981:** GJH's company timeline identifies MILI as its second house brand. [1]
- **2004:** GJH's timeline records expansion into health through Borsch Med; the brand history states that Borsch Med was registered in 2004. [1][8]

The company timeline places GOGO under 1982, while the GOGO page states 1986. This discrepancy remains unresolved; neither date should be presented as verified without confirmation. [1][4]

## Regional Presence

GJH says its distribution network serves markets across Asia Pacific. Its examples include Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, China, Hong Kong, Thailand, Brunei, Vietnam, Cambodia, and the Philippines. This is not evidence that each product or brand is currently available in every market. [1]

## Contact

GJH lists its Singapore office at **8 Gul Avenue, Singapore 629652** and provides an enquiry form and a Malaysia office address on its official contact page. For current contact details and business enquiries, use the [official contact page](https://www.gjh.com.sg/contact-us/). [9]

## New Moon — Deeper Research

Research reviewed on 11 November 2026. This section records public-source evidence and editorial implications, not legal ownership certification or an independent product-quality assessment.

### Company, Brand and Product

GJH's company timeline identifies New Moon as a house brand; its brand page describes New Moon as managed and distributed by GJH, with abalone as the flagship category. Distinguish the corporate enterprise, the consumer brand and individual products. A GJH corporate profile should explain the business portfolio and distribution capabilities; a New Moon brand page should explain products, occasions and brand heritage. [1][2]

### Historical Nuance

New Moon's official history dates the brand to 1959. A GJH-attributed feature hosted by The Edge Singapore says it joined GJH's portfolio in the mid-1980s. A 19 January 2023 Vulcan Post article, explicitly marked as sponsored, describes an acquisition in the 1980s. These sources distinguish brand establishment from later portfolio ownership; no acquisition agreement or precise transaction date was verified. The Edge feature is company-attributed material, not independent confirmation. [7][10][11]

Safe wording: "Established in 1959, New Moon is a food and beverage brand managed and distributed by Goh Joo Hin." Avoid asserting that GJH founded or acquired New Moon in 1959 without further evidence. [2][7]

### Product Scope and Origin

The official history records expansion into bird's nest in 2004, chicken essence in 2005, fragrant rice in 2006, oils/sauces in 2007, chilled/frozen foods in 2010 and pastes in 2011. [7]

The official e-shop's brand collection displayed 99 results at the time of review, spanning food, pantry and wellness-positioned items. That is a page-result count, not an audited global SKU total. `newmoon.com.sg` redirected to this collection during the review. [12]

Abalone listings include New Zealand, Australia, South Africa and Mexico. Product-origin claims must be tied to the specific SKU; a Singapore brand is not evidence that all its products are made in Singapore. A New Zealand 425g product page lists New Zealand origin and shellfish/sulphite allergens. [13][14]

### Trust Claims and Evidence Limits

| Claim | Evidence and editorial treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Best-selling abalone | GJH attributes a 13-year first-place claim to AC Nielsen surveys covering 1999–2011. No current Nielsen report was obtained; retain the period and attribution, or omit the claim. [2] |
| Historical awards | GJH lists dated Superbrands, brand and packaging awards. The awarding bodies' original records were not independently verified in this review; do not present these as current blanket certifications. [2][15] |
| Halal | Selected product listings identify Halal status. This does not establish certification for every New Moon item, facility or market; a current certificate was not verified. [13] |
| Government listing | Singapore's HPB supplier directory lists GJH and specific New Moon products. This is product/supplier evidence, not blanket government endorsement of the company or every product. [16] |
| Regional reach | GJH describes Asia-Pacific distribution. Brand registrations, distribution networks and current product availability are different claims and should not be conflated. [1][2] |

The OG retailer's New Moon description repeats much of GJH's brand copy, so it is not independent corroboration of the historical sales claim. A FairPrice product listing does provide separate evidence of a retail listing for a specific New Moon product; it does not prove nationwide availability or current sales leadership. [17][18]

### Business and Website Implications — Analysis

The useful brand narrative has three layers: heritage as context, named product ranges as concrete value, and specific sourcing/product evidence as trust. Premium presentation should make differences between products understandable rather than relying on decorative luxury cues.

Recommended content priorities are product-family navigation, official packaging photographs, origin/size/preparation/allergen details at SKU level, gifting use cases and a clear path to the official shop. Corporate-partnership enquiries should remain distinct from consumer shopping. These are design recommendations, not measured conversion results.

Do not publish unqualified leadership claims, product-wide health benefits, all-products certification badges, a single origin for the entire brand, or historical celebrity relationships described as current without fresh evidence. Product imagery must preserve labels and packaging; the prior generated enhancement was not adopted because it altered packaging details.
