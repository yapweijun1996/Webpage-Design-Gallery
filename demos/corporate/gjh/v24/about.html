Our Story

In the 1940s, the Goh brothers opened a provision store on Rochor Road.

New Moon began in 1959 with abalone as its first product. The present company, Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd, was incorporated on 23 September 1993 (UEN 199306282E).

Today, Goh Joo Hin develops food brands and distributes goods across food, electronics and health.

Sources: GJH company history, New Moon history and ACRA corporate entities dataset.

Our journey

  1. 1940s

    Rochor Road

    The Goh brothers opened a provision store on Rochor Road in Singapore.

  2. 1959

    New Moon

    New Moon began with abalone as its first product.

  3. 1981

    MILI

    MILI became GJH’s second house brand.

  4. 1982

    An export milestone

    GJH groups GOGO and Flying Wheel under its 1982 export milestone.

    The brand pages give different dates: GOGO, 1986 and Flying Wheel, 2004.

  5. 1993

    Company incorporation

    Goh Joo Hin Pte Ltd was incorporated on 23 September.

  6. 2004

    Borsch Med

    GJH expanded into health through Borsch Med.

  7. 2006

    Gigaset

    GJH moved into cordless phones with Gigaset.

  8. 2010

    Distribution and the Big Walk

    GJH records distribution milestones for Hormel, SPAM and California Mexico, while GJH and North East CDC organised the New Moon Big Walk and set up a S$200,000 healthcare fund for seniors.

  9. 2011

    Sony

    GJH added Sony personal entertainment products to its distribution portfolio.

  10. 2016

    GP Batteries

    GJH became a distributor for GP Batteries.

  11. 2018

    Casio and LG

    GJH records two milestones that year: Casio musical instruments and LG home entertainment.

  12. 2021

    Aeries

    GJH introduced Aeries air purifiers.

  13. 2025

    SteelSeries

    GJH reports a distribution partnership for SteelSeries gaming peripherals.

Sources: GJH company history, New Moon history, ACRA corporate entities dataset and CDC FY2010 annual report, p. 14.
