- Local file: `posable-right-hand.glb`, 506,696 bytes, SHA-256 `4e9a6219f4147f80fbd29cd4c349e1ae1d59d436d81822a0da8247984cb2d4e9`.
- The original GLB is kept byte for byte. Mini Street poses its 16-bone skeleton in JavaScript and adds a sleeve at runtime. The source has an untextured skin material and no bundled animation clips. Mini Street adds original procedural vertex colours for skin tone, creases and nails. A 256 × 256 procedural relief map supplies pores and fine skin lines, and nail plates have their own roughness. No additional hand assets are fetched.
