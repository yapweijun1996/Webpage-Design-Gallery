Teachers’
field notes.

Take a class from noticing an object to interpreting a living tradition. Use these prompts before, during and after the visit.

Open the lesson↓
Warm-up for the class

“This bowl has a wide rim. Someone used it for soup.”

Which claim needs evidence?
You can see the rim. The claim about soup needs another source, such as the maker or the person who used the bowl.
01 / Lesson sequence

Observe, infer, revisit.

01

Before the visit

Show the class a bowl. Ask which facts they can observe and which claims would need evidence.

02

At the kiln

Invite pupils to trace the uphill shape with their eyes, then compare it with the curve of a piece of pottery.

03

Back in class

Ask each pupil to write one observation, one inference and one question for a craft practitioner.
