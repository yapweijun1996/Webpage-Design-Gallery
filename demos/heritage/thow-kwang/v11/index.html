Dictionary / A–W

Look up a term.

AAsh
Fine residue carried from burning wood; it can settle on the wares during firing.
CChamber
The space that holds the pottery while it is being fired.
CChimney
The outlet at the uphill end of the kiln through which gases leave.
CClay body
The base ceramic material from which a vessel is made.
FFirebox
The area where the main fire begins, at the lower end of a climbing kiln.
GGlaze
A coating that forms a glassy surface in the firing.
PProfile
The outline of a vessel viewed from the side.
WWheel throwing
Making a vessel by centring, opening and lifting clay on a turning wheel.
