Accession notes / Three forms

A reader for silhouettes.

01

Bowl

An open rim gives the wide interior much of its identity. Look for the relationship between the rim diameter and the small foot.

02

Jar

The shoulder changes the silhouette. Compare where the widest part sits relative to the mouth and base.

03

Cup

Follow the sides from the foot to the lip. Is the vessel straight, flared or softly rounded?
