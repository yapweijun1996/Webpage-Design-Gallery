Heritage field guide / No. 0185 Lorong Tawas · Singapore
Plate 01 / Anatomy

How the kiln breathes.

The kiln is one climbing chamber, not separate rooms. Select a numbered part to read its role.

Diagram key / Select a part

01 · Firebox
At the lower end, the fire is started with wood. Follow the dotted path in the diagram: the heat travels uphill from here.
02 · Firing chamber
Pots sit in the long, rising chamber. Their position changes what the flame and drifting wood ash leave on each surface.
03 · Chimney
At the higher end, the chimney gives smoke and hot gases a way out. Its position helps draw the heat through the chamber.

Heritage record

From workshop to living heritage.

1940

A kiln on the slope

Thow Kwang’s tour guide dates construction of the kiln to 1940. It later produced household wares.

1965

A family takes over

The Tan family began operating the pottery business in 1965. The kiln is still a focus of their public heritage work.

2020

Craft recognised

The National Heritage Board recognised the kiln’s stewards in its 2020 intangible cultural heritage awards.
