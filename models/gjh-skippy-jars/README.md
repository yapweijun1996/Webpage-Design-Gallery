# SKIPPY S08 500 g jar form studies

[Editable Blender source](skippy-jars.blend) · [Generator](generate.py) · [Shared geometry](../../scripts/gjh_interiors/skippy_jars.py) · [Installed room detail GLB](../../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/interiors/s08-skippy-details.glb)

The two GJH listings share a nominal 73 × 73 × 124 mm envelope. Each unprinted jar has a rounded heel, narrowed shoulder and a distinct narrow lip under the ribbed screw-cap skirt. Cyan-blue and deeper blue pigments distinguish Creamy and Chunky. Profile measurements, closure details and exact number of cap ribs are visual estimates.
