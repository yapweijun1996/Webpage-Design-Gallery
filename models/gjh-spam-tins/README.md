# SPAM S09 340 g tin form studies

[Editable Blender source](spam-tins.blend) · [Generator](generate.py) · [Shared geometry](../../scripts/gjh_interiors/spam_tins.py) · [Installed room detail GLB](../../demos/corporate/gjh/v11/assets/interiors/s09-spam-details.glb)

The catalogue records a nominal 100 × 56 × 83 mm envelope for each of the three 340 g products. The unprinted form studies share rounded rectangular corners, projecting upper and lower rolls, a recessed top panel and an offset, elongated open pull tab. Each tin has its own original neutral body pigment. Corner radii, seam section, exact tab dimensions and the base are approximations.
