# MILI Vegetarian Mock Duck tin study

The [MILI Vegetarian Mock Duck 280 g catalogue entry](https://www.gjheshop.com/product/mili-vegetarian-mock-duck-280g/) identifies the sample. The [official front photograph](https://www.gjheshop.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/a08ee787192ce59100d8378c5118b4a8.jpg) shows straight sides and gold-toned rolled rims. Its view includes part of the top ellipse. The short tin in the earlier concept does not match the photographed outline.

The original unprinted study is 76 mm in diameter and 113 mm tall in scene metres. These are authored display dimensions. The retail dimensions, exact end tooling, complete wrap and print revision still require product evidence. The mesh uses a muted blue vertex finish and unprinted end surfaces. The photograph is linked for provenance and is not part of the asset.
